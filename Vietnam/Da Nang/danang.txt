The 30 questions, 4 answers each

1. Dragon Bridge. How long is the Dragon Bridge?
A) 450 m  B) 666 m  C) 1,850 m  D) 122.7 m

2. Carp-turns-dragon statue. The Dragon Bridge opened to traffic on 29 March, the anniversary of the Liberation of Da Nang. In which year?
A) 2000  B) 2009  C) 2017  D) 2013

3. Museum of Cham Sculpture. The Kingdom of Champa, whose sculpture this museum shows, was established in which year?
A) 192 AD  B) 875 AD  C) 979 AD  D) 1000 AD

4. Da Nang Cathedral. French troops landed in Da Nang in August of which year, ostensibly over the persecution of Catholic missionaries?
A) 1847  B) 1860  C) 1858  D) 1889

5. Hàn Market. The old name Cửa Hàn, from which the French "Tourane" derives, means the mouth of what?
A) The Hải Vân Pass  B) The Thu Bồn River  C) The Hàn River  D) The Perfume River

6. Han River Bridge. The Han River Bridge, the only swing bridge in Vietnam, opened on 29 March of which year?
A) 2000  B) 1997  C) 2009  D) 2013

7. Da Nang Museum. In which year did Da Nang separate from Quảng Nam province and become a centrally governed city?
A) 1975  B) 2000  C) 2005  D) 1997

8. Điện Hải Citadel ruins. The Điện Hải Citadel was first built as an earthen fortress in which year?
A) 1535  B) 1813  C) 1858  D) 1889

9. Da Nang Fine Arts Museum. Emperor Minh Mạng’s 1835 edict let European ships land only at Đà Nẵng. Whose port did Đà Nẵng supersede as the largest in the central region?
A) Huế’s  B) Quy Nhơn’s  C) Hội An’s  D) Vinh’s

10. Cồn Market. The American brig Franklin of Salem reached Da Nang, then called Turon, on 18 June of which year?
A) 1535  B) 1819  C) 1823  D) 1847

11. Da Nang railway station. Da Nang station first opened in which year?
A) 1902  B) 1889  C) 1905  D) 1975

12. Trưng Vương Theatre. In 1889 the French renamed the city. What name did they use?
A) Faifo  B) Turon  C) Kean  D) Tourane

13. Phổ Đà Pagoda. The name Đà Nẵng is thought to come from the Cham word da nak. What does it mean?
A) Source of the clam  B) Opening of a large river  C) City of the lion  D) Harbour of the dragon

14. Phước Ninh communal house. After the August Revolution of 1945 the city was briefly named after Thái Phiên. In which year did he lead a revolt, the Duy Tân Resistance?
A) 1858  B) 1930  C) 1945  D) 1916

15. Hero Statue. During the Vietnam War the air base at Da Nang averaged about how many aircraft operations a day?
A) 2,595  B) 259  C) 1,020  D) 4,800

16. Military Region 5 Museum. The Marble Mountain Air Facility, built in Da Nang during the war, was constructed in which year?
A) 1954  B) 1972  C) 1965  D) 1985

17. Da Nang martyrs’ memorial. Da Nang fell to North Vietnamese forces on 29 March of which year?
A) 1954  B) 1965  C) 1975  D) 1986

18. Queen Cobra sculpture. The Bà Nà Hills resort, reached by a 5 km cable car, has its peak at what height?
A) 1,487 m  B) 487 m  C) 1,150 m  D) 2,150 m

19. Trần Thị Lý Bridge. Which Da Nang bridge is the longest suspension bridge in Vietnam?
A) Trần Thị Lý Bridge  B) Han River Bridge  C) Dragon Bridge  D) Thuận Phước Bridge

20. Buddha statue. The Non Nước stone-carving village, at the foot of the Marble Mountains, joined the national intangible heritage list in which year?
A) 1997  B) 2014  C) 2005  D) 2019

21. Lê Cơ Park. Which international summit did Da Nang host in 2017?
A) The G20  B) ASEAN-only summit  C) APEC  D) The Olympic Council of Asia

22. An Hải Pagoda. In 1860 the French left Da Nang after a siege by the Vietnamese army under which commander?
A) Hoàng Diệu  B) Nguyễn Tri Phương  C) Lê Hoàn  D) Rigault de Genouilly

23. An Hải parish church. António de Faria was one of the first Europeans to visit Da Nang. Which year did he anchor there?
A) 1535  B) 1650  C) 1819  D) 1858

24. Biển Đông Park. Which beach near Da Nang was an R&R destination for American troops?
A) Mỹ Khê Beach  B) Lăng Cô Beach  C) Cửa Đại Beach  D) Non Nước Beach

25. Mỹ Khê Pagoda. The first tram stretch of 1905 ran between Observatory Point and which place?
A) Faifo  B) Tourane Mỹ Khê  C) Marble Mountain  D) Hải Vân

26. Đầm Rong Park. Da Nang’s port system is the third largest in Vietnam, after Ho Chi Minh City and which other port?
A) Cam Ranh  B) Vũng Tàu  C) Quy Nhơn  D) Haiphong

27. Dove monument. The Hải Vân Tunnel, the longest in south-east Asia, opened in which year?
A) 2005  B) 1975  C) 1997  D) 2013

28. Thuận Phước Bridge. The Thuận Phước Bridge was completed on 19 July of which year?
A) 2000  B) 2005  C) 2009  D) 2013

29. Như Nguyệt Park. Da Nang borders Huế across which pass?
A) The Ngoạn Mục Pass  B) The Khau Phạ Pass  C) The Hải Vân Pass  D) The Ô Quy Hồ Pass

30. Hòa Cường church. How far south of Hanoi is Da Nang?
A) 764 km  B) 564 km  C) 964 km  D) 1,164 km
