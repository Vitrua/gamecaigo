The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Phu Văn Lâu. Phu Văn Lâu, where imperial edicts and exam results were displayed, was built in which year?
A) 1802  B) 1819  C) 1833  D) 1900

2. Ngọ Môn. Ngọ Môn is the main southern gate of the Imperial City. In which year was the Imperial City listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site?
A) 1975  B) 1999  C) 2003  D) 1993

3. Nine Holy Cannons. The Nine Holy Cannons were cast by Huế artisans in the second year of Gia Long’s reign. Which year was that?
A) 1803  B) 1802  C) 1804  D) 1833

4. Trường Tiền Bridge. Trường Tiền Bridge was inaugurated on 18 December of which year?
A) 1885  B) 1897  C) 1900  D) 1930

5. Phan Bội Châu monument. French agents seized Phan Bội Châu in 1925. In which city?
A) Tokyo  B) Hanoi  C) Shanghai  D) Canton

6. Đông Ba Market. In which year did Emperor Thành Thái order the Đông Ba Market rebuilt at its current location?
A) 1899  B) 1885  C) 1887  D) 1930

7. Quốc Học – Huế High School. Quốc Học – Huế was founded on 23 October of which year?
A) 1802  B) 1908  C) 1945  D) 1896

8. Huế railway station. Which colonial authority built Huế railway station?
A) The Portuguese  B) The French authorities  C) The British  D) The Japanese

9. Trịnh Công Sơn Park. Trịnh Công Sơn, the songwriter who grew up in Huế, was born in which year?
A) 1925  B) 1949  C) 1939  D) 1957

10. Vĩ Dạ Bridge. Into which lagoon does the Perfume River empty?
A) The Cầu Hai lagoon  B) The Tam Giang lagoon  C) The Lập An lagoon  D) West Lake

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B

Sources and fun facts

1. Phu Văn Lâu: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_City,_Huế (Phu Văn Pavilion: built in 1819 before the imperial examinations)
   Fun fact: In 1829 Emperor Minh Mạng used it for an elephant-and-tiger fight and a three-day birthday banquet.
2. Ngọ Môn: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_City,_Huế (lead: designated in 1993 as part of the Complex of Huế Monuments)
   Fun fact: The Imperial City was begun in 1804 under Emperor Gia Long and enlarged to its present scale in 1833.
3. Nine Holy Cannons: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_City,_Huế (Nine imperial grand cannons: cast in 1803)
   Fun fact: They are considered the largest cannons in Vietnam and among its most valuable bronze works.
4. Trường Tiền Bridge: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trường_Tiền_Bridge (History: inauguration 18 December 1900)
   Fun fact: Eiffel’s company later replaced its rusted iron beams.
5. Phan Bội Châu monument: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phan_Bội_Châu (lead)
   Fun fact: He spent the rest of his life under house arrest in Huế and died in 1940.
6. Đông Ba Market: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Đông_Ba_Market (History)
   Fun fact: The market covers 47,614 square metres on the north bank of the Perfume River.
7. Quốc Học – Huế High School: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quoc_Hoc_High_School (lead: founded 23 October 1896)
   Fun fact: It is the third oldest high school in Vietnam; Hồ Chí Minh was dismissed from it in 1908.
8. Huế railway station: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hue_Railway_Station (lead)
   Fun fact: It is considered one of the most beautiful stations in Vietnam and stands on the North–South main line.
9. Trịnh Công Sơn Park: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trịnh_Công_Sơn (lead: born 28 February 1939)
   Fun fact: He was born in Buôn Ma Thuột and studied at the Lycée Français in Huế.
10. Vĩ Dạ Bridge: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfume_River (the river empties into the Tam Giang lagoon)
   Fun fact: In autumn, flowers from orchards upriver fall in the water, giving the river its fragrant name.
