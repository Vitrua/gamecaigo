The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Japanese Covered Bridge. Japanese merchants began building the covered bridge in 1593. In which year was it finished?
A) 1653  B) 1595  C) 1719  D) 1817

2. Phúc Kiến. How many Chinese assembly halls did Chinese immigrants build in Hội An?
A) Two  B) Eight  C) Twelve  D) Five

3. Museum of Hội An. Before Champa rule, the land of Hội An belonged to which prehistoric culture?
A) The Sa Huỳnh culture  B) The Đông Sơn culture  C) The Óc Eo culture  D) The Hòa Bình culture

4. Cẩm Phô Communal House. From which century did the Kingdom of Champa claim the area of Hội An?
A) The 6th century  B) The 10th century  C) The 2nd century  D) The 15th century

5. Hội An Catholic Church. In the 19th century the French introduced which type of building to the town’s streets?
A) Neo-Gothic cathedrals  B) Railway depots  C) French-style townhouses  D) Concrete apartment blocks

6. Hội An Confucian Temple. On 12 December of which year was the Old Town listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site?
A) 1999  B) 1985  C) 1993  D) 2009

7. An Hội statue garden. How many preserved houses does the Old Town contain?
A) 360  B) 680  C) 2,400  D) 1,068

8. Hội An Park. Hội An lies downstream on which river?
A) The Hương River  B) The Thu Bồn River  C) The Hàn River  D) The Mekong

9. Moonlight Bridge. In which year was the Old Town classified as a Special National Cultural Heritage Site?
A) 1985  B) 1999  C) 2009  D) 2003

10. Bảo Thắng Pagoda. During the 19th century the port of Hội An declined as which city developed its ports?
A) Huế  B) Da Nang  C) Quy Nhơn  D) Nha Trang

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B
