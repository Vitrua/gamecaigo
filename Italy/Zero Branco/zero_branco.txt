The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta. Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta: in which year was the parish church rebuilt and reconsecrated, on 17 August?
A) 1152  B) 1642  C) 1696  D) 1847

2. Fountain Via Noalese. Fountain Via Noalese: in the 2nd century BC the area around Zero was placed under the municipium of which Roman town?
A) Aquileia  B) Patavium  C) Opitergium  D) Altinum

3. Biblioteca Gaetano Cozzi. Biblioteca Gaetano Cozzi: in which year did Zero pass from the free Comune of Treviso to the Scaligeri?
A) 1312  B) 1152  C) 1388  D) 1866

4. Park at Pastrello. Park at Pastrello: in which year did the municipality take the name Zero Branco, after the annexation to the Kingdom of Italy?
A) 1797  B) 1853  C) 1866  D) 1984

5. Park at Macello. Park at Macello: in which year did construction of the bell tower of the parish church begin?
A) 1642  B) 1759  C) 1696  D) 1847

6. Playground Via Baden Powell. Playground Via Baden Powell: in which year was the organ of the parish church built by Giovanni Placa?
A) 1759  B) 1695  C) 1696  D) 1857

7. Chiesa di Sant’Alberto. Chiesa di Sant’Alberto: in 1807 Zero and Sant’Alberto became municipalities in the canton of Noale, in a department whose capital was which city?
A) Padua  B) Treviso  C) Venice  D) Vicenza

8. Chapel at Via Corniani. Chapel at Via Corniani: until 1853 Zero belonged to which province?
A) Venice  B) Padua  C) Vicenza  D) Belluno

9. Parco di Via Pacinotti. Parco di Via Pacinotti: Zero was chosen by Venetian nobles for summer residences thanks to its position halfway between Treviso and which city?
A) Padua  B) Vicenza  C) Venice  D) Belluno

10. Chiesa di Scandolara. Chiesa di Scandolara: under Venice, Zero alone passed to the podesteria of which town, while Sant’Alberto and Scandolara stayed under Treviso?
A) Padua  B) Mestre  C) Noale  D) Castelfranco

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B
