The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa arcipretale di San Martino Vescovo. In which year was the church of San Martino in Paese declared arcipretale?
A) 1603  B) 1793  C) 1918  D) 1957

2. Villa La Quiete. The chapel absorbed into Villa La Quiete, now the town hall, is dedicated to which title?
A) Santa Lucia  B) Madonna delle Grazie  C) Maternità di Maria Vergine  D) San Luca Evangelista

3. Villa Panizza. Which artist made the mosaics inside Villa Panizza?
A) Berto Rossato  B) Angelo Gatto  C) Gian Maria Lepscky  D) Bartolomeo Polito

4. Oratorio della Madonna delle Grazie. In the Middle Ages, Sovernigo and Paese belonged to the pieve of which village?
A) Quinto  B) Istrana  C) Postioma  D) Cornuda

5. Stazione di Paese. On which railway line is the main railway station of Paese?
A) Montebelluna-Treviso  B) Vicenza-Treviso  C) Treviso-Ostiglia  D) Venezia-Udine

6. Bus stop "Paese Municipio". Casa Quaglia, a Gothic building on Via Roma, is among the oldest villa-type buildings of the province of Treviso. From which century does it date?
A) 15th century  B) 13th century  C) 17th century  D) 19th century

7. Drinking-water fountain, Via delle Levade. Where is the lowest point of Paese, at 19 m above sea level?
A) Near the Montello border in the north  B) South of the main town, near the border with Quinto  C) On the Piave bank  D) Next to Postioma

8. Park at Case Corrente. Which state road, also called "Castellana", passes tangent to the centre of Paese and links it to Treviso?
A) SS 13 Pontebbana  B) SS 14 Triestina  C) SS 53 Postumia  D) SS 348 Feltrina

9. Colmello bus stop. In which year was the former municipality of Padernello merged into Paese?
A) 1867  B) 1797  C) 1815  D) 1946

10. Bus stop "Paese Hotel Oasi". Francesco Baracca’s squadron airfield in the First World War was set up on the border between Paese and which municipality?
A) Istrana  B) Quinto di Treviso  C) Treviso  D) Ponzano Veneto

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-C, 3-D, 4-A, 5-B, 6-A, 7-B, 8-C, 9-A, 10-B

Sources and fun facts

1. Chiesa arcipretale di San Martino Vescovo: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Chiesa arcipretale di San Martino Vescovo)
   Fun fact: Its marble façade was added in 1957, and its baptismal font came from the old church of Possagno, where Antonio Canova was baptised.
2. Villa La Quiete: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Architetture civili, Villa "La Quiete")
   Fun fact: Villa La Quiete began life as a country casino before becoming the municipal seat.
3. Villa Panizza: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Architetture civili, Villa Panizza)
   Fun fact: The villa also houses the municipal library and its park hosts events such as the Paese Jazz Festival (wanderlog.com).
4. Oratorio della Madonna delle Grazie: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Il medioevo)
   Fun fact: Quinto, today a separate municipality, once headed the pieve with Paese, Castagnole, Sovernigo, Villa and Malzago.
5. Stazione di Paese: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stazione_di_Paese; it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Ferrovie)
   Fun fact: Paese has a second station with a similar name, Paese-Castagnole, on the Montebelluna-Treviso line.
6. Bus stop "Paese Municipio": it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Architetture civili, Casa Quaglia)
   Fun fact: Casa Quaglia is considered one of the first examples of a villa in the Trevigiano.
7. Drinking-water fountain, Via delle Levade: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Geografia fisica)
   Fun fact: The highest point, 70 m, lies in the north beyond Postioma, on the border with Volpago del Montello.
8. Park at Case Corrente: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Strade)
   Fun fact: The SS 348 Feltrina crosses only the hamlets of Castagnole and Postioma.
9. Colmello bus stop: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (L’Ottocento: 1 January 1867)
   Fun fact: The merger followed the annexation of the Veneto to the Kingdom of Italy.
10. Bus stop "Paese Hotel Oasi": it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Le due grandi guerre)
   Fun fact: On 18 March 1918 the XV Group arrived at Villa Onesti and stayed until February 1919.
