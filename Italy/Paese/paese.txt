Sources and fun facts

1. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Chiesa arcipretale di San Martino Vescovo)
   Fun fact: Its marble façade was added in 1957, and its baptismal font came from the old church of Possagno, where Antonio Canova was baptised.
2. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Architetture civili, Villa "La Quiete")
   Fun fact: Villa La Quiete began life as a country casino before becoming the municipal seat.
3. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Architetture civili, Villa Panizza)
   Fun fact: The villa also houses the municipal library and its park hosts events such as the Paese Jazz Festival (wanderlog.com).
4. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Il medioevo)
   Fun fact: Quinto, today a separate municipality, once headed the pieve with Paese, Castagnole, Sovernigo, Villa and Malzago.
5. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stazione_di_Paese; it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Ferrovie)
   Fun fact: Paese has a second station with a similar name, Paese-Castagnole, on the Montebelluna-Treviso line.
6. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Architetture civili, Casa Quaglia)
   Fun fact: Casa Quaglia is considered one of the first examples of a villa in the Trevigiano.
7. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Geografia fisica)
   Fun fact: The highest point, 70 m, lies in the north beyond Postioma, on the border with Volpago del Montello.
8. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Strade)
   Fun fact: The SS 348 Feltrina crosses only the hamlets of Castagnole and Postioma.
9. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (L’Ottocento: 1 January 1867)
   Fun fact: The merger followed the annexation of the Veneto to the Kingdom of Italy.
10. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paese_(Italia) (Le due grandi guerre)
   Fun fact: On 18 March 1918 the XV Group arrived at Villa Onesti and stayed until February 1919.
