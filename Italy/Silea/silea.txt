   Fun fact: Roman finds have turned up along its course.

   Fun fact: It was reconsecrated on 27 March 1859.

   Fun fact: The Barbaro family also built Villa Valier on the Sile.

   Fun fact: The bell tower dates from 1547 and its spire was added in 1891.
