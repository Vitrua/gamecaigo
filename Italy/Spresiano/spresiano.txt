The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Biblioteca Comunale di Spresiano. Biblioteca Comunale: in which year, according to Paul the Deacon, did the Lombard king Alboin meet Bishop Felix of Treviso at the Lovadina crossing?
A) 476  B) 569  C) 568  D) 774

2. Place of worship on Via Alessandro Manzoni. Place of worship on Via Alessandro Manzoni: the Pontebbana military road, rebuilt at the start of the 19th century, was ordered by which ruler?
A) Francis Joseph  B) Eugène de Beauharnais  C) Victor Emmanuel II  D) Napoleon

3. Chiesa della Santissima Trinità. Chiesa della Santissima Trinità: which viceroy won the 1809 battle fought on the Piave against the Austrians?
A) Eugène de Beauharnais  B) Joachim Murat  C) Radetzky  D) Archduke Charles

4. Cemetery of Spresiano. Cemetery of Spresiano: in the 15th century which canal crossed Lovadina, powering mills, forges and paper mills?
A) The Brentella  B) The Sile  C) The Piavesella  D) The Montegrappa canal

5. Park in Spresiano. Park in Spresiano: for centuries, which material was floated on rafts from Cadore and the Montello down the Piave?
A) Salt  B) Grain  C) Timber  D) Marble

6. Chiesa dell’Assunzione. Chiesa dell’Assunzione: in 958 the court of Lovadina was donated to Rambaldo of which family?
A) Collalto  B) Mocenigo  C) Corner  D) Da Camino

7. Chiesetta di San Giovanni, Lovadina. Chiesetta di San Giovanni, Lovadina: in 1021 the first chapel here was assigned to the abbey of San Zeno in which city?
A) Padua  B) Vicenza  C) Venice  D) Verona

8. Cemetery of Lovadina. Cemetery of Lovadina: the pilgrim road known as the Via Hungarica is named after repeated raids by whom?
A) The Huns  B) The Hungarians  C) The Turks  D) The Lombards

9. Chiesa di Visnadello. Chiesa di Visnadello: the Priula bridge on the Pontebbana reduced the importance of the older crossing of which river?
A) The Sile  B) The Livenza  C) The Piave  D) The Brenta

10. Villa Sina park. Villa Sina park: at the end of the 19th century Spresiano led Italy in which industry, around the Lazzaris plant?
A) Silk  B) Woodworking  C) Paper  D) Ceramics

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B
