The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa di San Cassiano. In which year is the name Quinto first cited, in a papal bull concerning the pieve of San Cassiano?
A) 992  B) 1152  C) 1317  D) 1758

2. Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio Martire. Who designed the post-war reconstruction of the parish church of San Giorgio?
A) Jacopo Guarana  B) Lattanzio Querena  C) Antonio Beni  D) Paolo Machiavelli

3. Chiesa di Santa Cristina. Which painter made the 1505 altarpiece kept in the church of Santa Cristina?
A) Pozzoserrato  B) Lorenzo Lotto  C) Jacopo Guarana  D) Angelo Gatto

4. Fiume Sile at Quinto. For centuries the economy of Quinto depended on the Sile river mostly through which activity?
A) Salt trade  B) Shipbuilding  C) Fishing  D) Milling

5. Villa Memo Giordani Valeri. A work that completed a triptych by Pozzoserrato is kept in the chapel of which villa in Quinto?
A) Villa Borghesan  B) Villa Giordani-Valeri  C) Villa Ciardi  D) Palazzo Lollin

6. Mulino di Cervara. Restoration of the Cervara mill, already working at the end of the 14th century, began in which year?
A) 1941  B) 1317  C) 1992  D) 1505

7. Giardino Pubblico Allende. The pilots of which First World War squadron, including Francesco Baracca, lodged in villas at Quinto?
A) 70th Squadron  B) 91st Squadron  C) 77th Squadron  D) 76th Squadron

8. Former Treviso-Ostiglia railway. Between 1941 and 1947, which railway line crossed Quinto and gave it its own station?
A) Venezia-Udine  B) Treviso-Belluno  C) Padova-Bassano  D) Treviso-Ostiglia

9. Capitello Mariano di Via Boiago. Which member of the band Pooh, a bassist, was born in Quinto’s Villa Borghesan?
A) Roby Facchinetti  B) Dodi Battaglia  C) Stefano D’Orazio  D) Red Canzian

10. Viewpoint at Via Cornarotta. About how many hectares does the Cervara oasis cover on its islet between the Sile and the Piovega canal?
A) 5 ha  B) 12 ha  C) 25 ha  D) 60 ha

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-C, 3-B, 4-D, 5-B, 6-C, 7-B, 8-D, 9-D, 10-C

Sources and fun facts

1. Chiesa di San Cassiano: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinto_di_Treviso (Storia: papal bull of Eugenius III, 3 May 1152)
   Fun fact: The name Quinto denotes an ancient mansio, a changing station five miles from Treviso.
2. Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio Martire: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinto_di_Treviso (Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio Martire)
   Fun fact: The old 14th-century church replaced the ancient pieve, once the centre of life shifted south toward the Sile.
3. Chiesa di Santa Cristina: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinto_di_Treviso (Chiesa di Santa Cristina; consecrated in 1933)
   Fun fact: The unusual bell tower sits above the entrance portal of the neo-Romanesque church.
4. Fiume Sile at Quinto: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinto_di_Treviso (Storia: attività molitoria)
   Fun fact: FAI notes the Sile is a spring-fed river whose flow stays nearly constant all year.
5. Villa Memo Giordani Valeri: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinto_di_Treviso (Chiesa parrocchiale di San Giorgio Martire)
   Fun fact: Palazzo Lollin, once the finest villa of the area and frescoed by Pozzoserrato, was destroyed in the 19th century.
6. Mulino di Cervara: magicoveneto.it https://www.magicoveneto.it/treviso/Sile/Oasi-Naturalistica-Mulino-Cervara-Parco-Fiume-Sile.htm; FAI https://fondoambiente.it/luoghi/il-corso-del-fiume-sile-dalle-sorgenti-alla-foce
   Fun fact: The mill sits on an islet between the Sile and the Piovega canal; the old mill is now an ethnographic museum.
7. Giardino Pubblico Allende: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinto_di_Treviso (Ville Venete: 91ª Squadriglia, "la Squadriglia degli Assi")
   Fun fact: Baracca lodged in villa Borghesan until his death; the villa was demolished in the 1950s after a small tornado.
8. Former Treviso-Ostiglia railway: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinto_di_Treviso (Storia)
   Fun fact: The old line is now a cycle path.
9. Capitello Mariano di Via Boiago: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinto_di_Treviso (Ville Venete)
   Fun fact: The villa was demolished in the 1950s after a small tornado.
10. Viewpoint at Via Cornarotta: FAI https://fondoambiente.it/luoghi/il-corso-del-fiume-sile-dalle-sorgenti-alla-foce
   Fun fact: Its springs are called risorgive, or "fontanassi" in Veneto dialect.
