A) 1918  B) 1866  C) 1917  D) 1944

7. Parco “Brigata Julia”. Parco “Brigata Julia”: the Julia Brigade belongs to which Italian army corps?
A) Bersaglieri  B) Paratroopers  C) Marines  D) Alpini
