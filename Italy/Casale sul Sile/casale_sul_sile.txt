The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta. Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta: which painter frescoed the ceiling of the 18th-century parish church?

A) 1170  B) 1418  C) 1944  D) 1101

3. Parco delle Vecchie Pioppe. Parco delle Vecchie Pioppe: in which year was the castle of Casale restored by Guido Canal, podestà of Treviso?
A) 1418  B) 1101  C) 1944  D) 1983

4. Parco Via Re Ashoka. Parco Via Re Ashoka: which lords of Treviso used the castle of Casale in their fight against the Venetians?
A) The Ezzelini  B) The Scaligeri  C) The da Camino  D) The Collalto

5. Park at San Michele. Park at San Michele: on the right bank of which river did the castle of Casale stand?
A) The Piave  B) The Zero  C) The Sile  D) The Brenta

6. Santuario della Natività della Beata Vergine. Santuario della Natività: whose tower appears on the coat of arms of Casale sul Sile?
A) The Carraresi  B) The da Camino  C) The Collalto  D) The Ezzelini

7. Chapel at Via Peschiere. Chapel at Via Peschiere: the old rectory next to the parish church of Casale is one of the oldest villas of the town. From which century does it date?
A) The 14th century  B) The 18th century  C) The 20th century  D) The 16th century

8. Playground Via Fornace. Playground Via Fornace: Villa Cornaro-Gabbianelli, in the hamlet of Lughignano, dates from the end of which century?
A) The 13th century  B) The 15th century  C) The 17th century  D) The 19th century

9. Park at Trabucco. Park at Trabucco: Villa Canossa, one of the villas around Casale, dates from which century?
A) The 14th century  B) The 15th century  C) The 17th century  D) The 19th century

10. Church at Via Belvedere. Church at Via Belvedere: in which year were the coat of arms and banner of Casale sul Sile granted by presidential decree?
A) 1944  B) 1983  C) 1101  D) 1418
