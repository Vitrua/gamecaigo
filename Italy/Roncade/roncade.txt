The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa di Ognissanti. Chiesa di Ognissanti: which architect from Vicenza designed the neoclassical façade of 1768?
A) Andrea Palladio  B) Ottavio Bertotti Scamozzi  C) Jacopo Sansovino  D) Baldassare Longhena

2. Chapel at Via Garibaldi. Chapel at Via Garibaldi: construction of the church of Ognissanti began in 1527. In which year was it completed?
A) 1748  B) 1768  C) 1866  D) 1566

3. Memorial to the fallen. Memorial to the fallen: in which year did President Ciampi grant Roncade the title of città?
A) 2004  B) 1866  C) 1956  D) 2010

4. Playground Via Fusana. Playground Via Fusana: in which year were Neolithic human remains, flint arrowheads and a knife found near the centre of Roncade?
A) 1866  B) 1956  C) 1937  D) 1992

5. Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista. Chiesa di San Giovanni Battista (Biancade): around the 10th century, which noble family of Lombard origin took possession of the territory of Roncade?
A) The Carraresi  B) The da Camino  C) The Collalto  D) The Ezzelini

6. Chapel at Via Ca’ Morelli. Chapel at Via Ca’ Morelli: the estates of which Venetian family gave rise to the hamlet of Ca’ Tron?
A) The Tron  B) The Morosini  C) The Badoer  D) The Pesaro

7. Chiesa dei Santi Cornelio e Cipriano. Chiesa dei Santi Cornelio e Cipriano: the pieve of San Cipriano depended on the monastery of San Cipriano on which island?
A) Burano  B) Torcello  C) Chioggia  D) Murano

8. Church at Santa Fosca. Church at Santa Fosca: which Roman road, built in 15 BC by the general Drusus, crossed the territory of Roncade?
A) The Via Annia  B) The Via Claudia Augusta Altinate  C) The Via Postumia  D) The Via Aemilia

9. Chiesa di Sant’Ulderico. Chiesa di Sant’Ulderico: the castle of Musestre was the residence of Eberardo of Friuli, father of which emperor?
A) Charlemagne  B) Otto I  C) Berengar of Friuli  D) Frederick Barbarossa

10. Church of Madonna della Salute. Church of Madonna della Salute: the Via Annia, which crossed Roncade, linked Patavium (Padua) with which important city?
A) Rome  B) Aquileia  C) Verona  D) Ravenna

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B
