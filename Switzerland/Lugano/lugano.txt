The 30 questions, 4 answers each

1. Cattedrale di San Lorenzo. In which year did the church of San Lorenzo become Lugano’s cathedral?
A) 818  B) 1888  C) 1078  D) 1517

2. Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angioli. Which Renaissance painter made the large Passion of Christ fresco in Santa Maria degli Angioli, built in 1499?
A) Leonardo da Vinci  B) Andrea Mantegna  C) Giovanni Segantini  D) Bernardino Luini

3. Hotel Splendide Royal. Which hotel on the lakefront preserves the original structure of Villa Merlina?
A) Splendide Royal  B) Grand Hotel Villa Castagnola  C) Hotel Walter au Lac  D) Hotel Lugano Dante

4. Museo delle Culture. Villa Malpensata, on the lakefront, houses which museum?
A) Museo di storia naturale  B) Museo Hermann Hesse  C) Museo delle Culture  D) Museo della pesca

5. Tempietto di George Washington. The little temple on the lakefront, built in 1859, honours which historical figure?
A) Giuseppe Garibaldi  B) Napoleon  C) George Washington  D) Wilhelm Tell

6. Chiesa di Santa Maria di Loreto. In which quarter of Lugano does the church of Santa Maria di Loreto stand, on a hill overlooking the city?
A) Loreto  B) Pambio  C) Besso  D) Cassarate

7. Torre Enderlin. The tower at the top of Parco Tassino, a former Enderlin hunting reserve, is recognisable by which colour?
A) White  B) Black  C) Green  D) Pink

8. Via Nassa. What kind of street is Via Nassa, in the old town?
A) A Roman bridge  B) An old arcaded merchant street  C) A funicular track  D) A fortified wall

9. Palazzo Civico. In which year was the Palazzo Civico, seat of the city government, built on Piazza della Riforma?
A) 1512  B) 1803  C) 1844  D) 1912

10. Monumento all’indipendenza del Ticino. Which act of 1803 created the canton of Ticino by uniting the cantons of Lugano and Bellinzona?
A) The Federal Pact  B) The Act of Mediation  C) The Treaty of Westphalia  D) The Helvetic Constitution

11. Guglielmo Tell memorial. In which year did the Swiss Confederation conquer Lugano for good?
A) 1512  B) 724  C) 1441  D) 1798

12. Fondazione Filippo Ciani. Giacomo and Filippo Ciani had their neoclassical villa built in 1840. Which architect designed it?
A) Mario Botta  B) Rino Tami  C) Ivano Gianola  D) Luigi Clerichetti

13. Villa Ciani. In which year did the city of Lugano buy Villa Ciani and its grounds to turn them into a public park?
A) 1840  B) 1912  C) 1868  D) 1968

14. Mouth of the Cassarate river. Parco Ciani extends to the mouth of which river?
A) Tresa  B) Vedeggio  C) Magliasina  D) Cassarate

15. La Desolazione. Which sculptor created "La Desolazione", the best-known sculpture of Parco Ciani?
A) Vincenzo Vela  B) Medardo Rosso  C) Alberto Giacometti  D) Auguste Rodin

16. Chiesa riformata. Which two figures of the Italian Risorgimento were guests of the Ciani brothers in the park?
A) Garibaldi and Cavour  B) Verdi and Manzoni  C) Giuseppe Mazzini and Carlo Cattaneo  D) Mazzini and Garibaldi

17. Agorateca library. Who designed Lugano’s cantonal library (1941), next to the Palazzo degli Studi?
A) Mario Botta  B) Ivano Gianola  C) Rino Tami  D) Luigi Clerichetti

18. Parco San Michele. Parco San Michele, with Mediterranean vegetation, holds allegorical sculptures dedicated to what?
A) The human senses  B) The four seasons  C) The Swiss cantons  D) The cardinal virtues

19. Funicolare Monte Brè. What is the height of Monte Brè, reachable by funicular from Castagnola-Cassarate?
A) 514 m  B) 1,200 m  C) 2,100 m  D) 925 m

20. Chiesa di San Giorgio, Castagnola. Into how many administrative quarters (quartieri) is the city of Lugano divided?
A) 9  B) 21  C) 14  D) 30

21. Parco dell’Heleneum. Stone Age tools have been found in Castagnola and which other quarters?
A) Paradiso, Melide and Bissone  B) Agno, Magliaso and Caslano  C) Breganzona, Davesco and Gandria  D) Sorengo, Massagno and Vezia

22. Villa Favorita park. Lugano was cantonal capital in rotation with Bellinzona and Locarno. In which period was it capital for the first time in the 19th century?
A) 1803-1809  B) 1827-1833  C) 1845-1851 only  D) 1863-1869 only

23. Parco degli Ulivi. The oratory of San Michele in Ruvigliana was built on the remains of what?
A) An ancient castle  B) A Roman temple  C) A watermill  D) A customs house

24. Viewpoint Via Baré. Which mountain, 1,516 m high, can be reached by paths from Monte Brè?
A) Monte Generoso  B) Monte Tamaro  C) Monte Lema  D) Monte Boglia

25. Parco di Viganello. Tombs found in Pregassona and Viganello show contacts with which two ancient civilizations?
A) Egyptian and Greek  B) Etruscan and Celtic  C) Phoenician and Roman  D) Viking and Frankish

26. Playground Via al Boschetto. Roman Luganum was an important trading point on which road linking Milan with the Alpine regions?
A) Via Francigena  B) Via Appia  C) Via Claudia Augusta  D) Via Varesina

27. Chiesa di San Giuseppe. In which year were the Statutes of Lugano, modelled on those of Milan, issued?
A) 1441  B) 984  C) 1286  D) 1512

28. Chiesa della Santissima Trinità. The opening of which railway in 1882 helped turn Lugano into a tourist and service centre?
A) The Simplon railway  B) The Rhaetian Railway  C) The Gotthard railway  D) The Bernina railway

29. Parco Villa Saroli. Lugano is which rank among Swiss cities by population?
A) Second  B) Fourth  C) Ninth  D) Fifteenth

30. Piazza Molino Nuovo. The oratory of San Maurizio in Molino Nuovo is attested from which year (its small cemetery was designed in 1935 by Mario Chiattone)?
A) 1203  B) 1099  C) 1335  D) 1499

Sources and fun facts

1. Cattedrale di San Lorenzo: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano_Cathedral (History: collegiate church 1078, cathedral 1888)
   Fun fact: Its marble façade was completed in 1517 and was inspired by the Certosa di Pavia.
2. Chiesa di Santa Maria degli Angioli: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Architetture religiose)
   Fun fact: The church was built by the Observant Franciscan friars.
3. Hotel Splendide Royal: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Architetture civili: l’Hotel Splendide Royal conserva la struttura originaria di Villa Merlina)
   Fun fact: The nearby Villa Malpensata houses the Museo delle Culture.
4. Museo delle Culture: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Architetture civili)
   Fun fact: It stands near Riva Vela and Riva Caccia, with public gardens and monuments.
5. Tempietto di George Washington: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Architetture civili: Tempietto di George Washington, 1859)
   Fun fact: It was built by Abbondio Chialiva and sculpted by Angelo Bruneri.
6. Chiesa di Santa Maria di Loreto: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Architetture religiose)
   Fun fact: Loreto is one of Lugano’s 21 quarters.
7. Torre Enderlin: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Aree naturali: Torre Enderlin, riconoscibile per il colore rosato)
   Fun fact: The park became city property in 1970 and hosts a colony of fallow deer and mouflons.
8. Via Nassa: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Architetture civili: Via Nassa, antico porticato mercantile)
   Fun fact: Today it is the city’s main shopping street.
9. Palazzo Civico: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Architetture civili: costruito nel 1844 su progetto di Luigi Clerichetti)
   Fun fact: It is neoclassical, with a portico and sculpted coats of arms.
10. Monumento all’indipendenza del Ticino: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Storia: Atto di Mediazione del 1803)
   Fun fact: Lugano later alternated with Bellinzona and Locarno as cantonal capital.
11. Guglielmo Tell memorial: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Storia: età moderna)
   Fun fact: Lugano then became the seat of a bailiwick governed by the thirteen sovereign cantons.
12. Fondazione Filippo Ciani: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parco_Ciani and it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Aree naturali)
   Fun fact: Luigi Clerichetti also designed the Palazzo Civico (1844).
13. Villa Ciani: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parco_Ciani (Storia)
   Fun fact: On the same spot the Rusca of Como had built a castle in 1286.
14. Mouth of the Cassarate river: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parco_Ciani (Posizione)
   Fun fact: At the river mouth there is a panoramic belvedere with a small beach.
15. La Desolazione: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parco_Ciani (Header)
   Fun fact: Parco Ciani is the largest urban garden in Italian-speaking Switzerland.
16. Chiesa riformata: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Aree naturali: Parco Ciani)
   Fun fact: Lugano was a refuge for Italian exiles during the Risorgimento.
17. Agorateca library: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Architetture moderne)
   Fun fact: The adjacent Palazzo dei Congressi was designed by Rolf Georg Otto (1965-1975).
18. Parco San Michele: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Aree naturali)
   Fun fact: Its panoramic terrace looks over the gulf of Lugano toward the Po Plain.
19. Funicolare Monte Brè: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Brè (header)
   Fun fact: From the summit, paths lead to Monte Boglia and down to Gandria.
20. Chiesa di San Giorgio, Castagnola: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Suddivisioni amministrative; Castagnola-Cassarate-Ruvigliana is one of them)
   Fun fact: Many correspond to former independent municipalities.
21. Parco dell’Heleneum: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Storia: primi insediamenti)
   Fun fact: Copper and Iron Age traces show stable settlements later.
22. Villa Favorita park: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Storia: 1827–1833, 1845–1851, 1863–1869)
   Fun fact: Today Bellinzona is the sole cantonal capital.
23. Parco degli Ulivi: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano (Architetture religiose)
   Fun fact: Ruvigliana lies on the slopes of Monte Brè.
24. Viewpoint Via Baré: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Brè (Monumenti e luoghi d’interesse)
   Fun fact: Paths also lead to Alpe Bolla (1,129 m) and Cureggia (655 m).

   Fun fact: Lugano was surrounded by prehistoric settlements long before Roman times.
