A) 1171  B) 1579  C) 1607  D) 1830

3. Santuario della Madonna delle Rive. Santuario della Madonna delle Rive: in which year did Faido become the first town in Ticino with electric lighting?
A) 1889  B) 1820  C) 1881  D) 1905

4. Faido railway station. Faido railway station: in which years was the Gotthard Rail Tunnel built?

5. Church of San Siro. Church of San Siro: in which year is this church, once the baptistery of the Vicinanza, first mentioned?
A) 1331  B) 1459  C) 1171  D) 1579

A) Uri  B) Schwyz  C) Zurich  D) Bern

7. Church in Campello. Church in Campello: in which years was the Gotthard road built?
A) 1331–1340  B) 1607–1608  C) 1871–1881  D) 1820–1830

8. Parish church of SS. Lorenzo e Agata. Parish church of SS. Lorenzo e Agata: the gorge of which mountain was a barrier that made Faido a stop for travellers?
A) Mount Generoso  B) Mount Piottino  C) Mount Tamaro  D) Mount Brè

9. Centro Scolastico bus stop. Centro Scolastico bus stop: which family held the patronage of the chapel of St. Bernardino of Siena (1459) and owned a six-storey tower house?
A) The Orelli family  B) The Rusca family  C) The Varesi family  D) The Stanga family

10. Ospedale Distrettuale bus stop. Ospedale Distrettuale bus stop: in which year was the Hôtel Suisse founded?
A) 1820  B) 1905  C) 1881  D) 1889
