The 30 questions, 4 answers each

1. Castello Visconteo. In which year did the Visconti occupy and enlarge the castle first mentioned in 1210 as Orelli Castle?
A) 1156  B) 1342  C) 1503  D) 1531

2. Collegiata di Sant’Antonio Abate. The current parish church of Sant’Antonio Abate replaced a first church of 1353–1354. In which year?
A) 1424  B) 1742  C) 1884  D) 1664

3. Piazza Grande. The Locarno Film Festival holds open-air screenings on the Piazza Grande in which month?
A) August  B) June  C) September  D) December

4. Church of Santa Caterina d’Alessandria. The church of Santa Caterina was attached to a monastery of which order, first mentioned in 1291?
A) The Capuchins  B) The Ursulines  C) The Humiliati  D) The Benedictines

5. Cantonal Library. The Locarno Treaties, a series of European territorial agreements, were negotiated here in October of which year?
A) 1815  B) 1919  C) 1925  D) 1946

6. Church of Santa Maria in Selva. Santa Maria in Selva was consecrated in which year (only its choir and bell tower remain since 1884)?
A) 1424  B) 1230  C) 1621  D) 1664

7. Parco San Carlo. Under the Treaty of Freiburg in which year did the Confederates receive all of Locarno?
A) 1503  B) 1513  C) 1798  D) 1516

8. Church of San Giovanni Battista. A large necropolis with over 200 graves was discovered at Solduno in which year?
A) 1934  B) 1935  C) 1946  D) 1995

9. Chapel of the Resurrection. A vision of the Virgin Mary by the Franciscan brother Bartolomeo d’Ivrea, which founded the Madonna del Sasso sanctuary above Locarno, took place in which year?
A) 1230  B) 1342  C) 1480  D) 1516

10. Church of the Santissima Trinità. The church of the Santissima Trinità dei Monti was consecrated in which year?
A) 1424  B) 1621  C) 1531  D) 1664

11. Church of the Sacra Famiglia. What is the highest point of the municipality of Locarno?
A) Cimetta, 1,474 m  B) Cardada, 1,340 m  C) Monte Verità, 1,100 m  D) Pizzo Vogorno, 2,442 m

12. Parco della Pace. At what elevation does the municipality of Locarno start, at the lake?
A) 109 m  B) 309 m  C) 409 m  D) 209 m

13. Saleggi primary school. In which year did the burghers of Locarno have their own administration, with grazing rights in Saleggi?
A) 1164  B) 1224  C) 1291  D) 1439

14. Fontana Pedrazzini. Frederick Barbarossa granted extensive market rights to Locarno in which year?
A) 1002  B) 1186  C) 1224  D) 1164

15. Istituto Ricerche Solari. Locarno is first mentioned in a document of 807 under which name?
A) Leocarni  B) Luggarus  C) Lucarius  D) Lucaris

16. Lido swimming pool. The town of Locarno is the third largest in Ticino, after Lugano and which other town?
A) Mendrisio  B) Chiasso  C) Bellinzona  D) Ascona

17. Via Vallemaggia fountain. How many graves were found in Via Valle Maggia in 1995 and 1997?
A) 14  B) 200  C) 57  D) 1,360

18. Centro Professionale Locarno. In which year was Locarno awarded to Count Franchino Rusca as a fief?
A) 1439  B) 1342  C) 1503  D) 1516

19. Planet path: Earth. The Locarno planet model starts at Via Gioacchino Respini. In which village is its last planet, Pluto, located?
A) Ascona  B) Orselina  C) Losone  D) Tegna

20. Planet path: Saturn. The planet model begins where the cycle path along which river starts?
A) The Ticino  B) The Maggia  C) The Verzasca  D) The Brenno

21. Planet path: small planets. Which town lies across the river Maggia from Locarno, on the south-western part of the delta?
A) Muralto  B) Minusio  C) Ascona  D) Brissago

22. Scuola media library. How many inhabitants does Locarno proper have, roughly?
A) 6,000  B) 16,000  C) 30,000  D) 56,000

23. Parco Privato. Casa Rusca, now the municipal art gallery, was built in which period?
A) The first half of the 18th century  B) The 13th century  C) The 16th century  D) The 20th century

24. Playground Parco Giochi Bosco Isolino. A defensive bastion added in 1507 to protect the castle entrance was possibly designed by whom?
A) Michelangelo  B) Filippo Brunelleschi  C) Andrea Palladio  D) Leonardo da Vinci

25. Playground Via San Jorio. How many urn graves were found near Via San Jorio in 1934, from the Early Bronze Age?
A) 7  B) 14  C) 57  D) 200

26. Lakefront Lungolago Motta fountain. Until which century was the Roman necropolis of Locarno used?
A) The 1st century BC  B) The 6th century AD  C) The 10th century AD  D) The mid-3rd century AD

27. Via Respini drinking fountain. In which year did the Confederates demolish much of Visconti Castle, leaving only the central core?
A) 1531  B) 1342  C) 1507  D) 1921

28. Sentiero al Tazzino fountain. Palazzo Casorella (Casa degli Orelli), north-east of the castle, dates from which century?
A) The 12th century  B) The 14th century  C) The 16th century  D) The 19th century

29. Via Pioda fountain. Since which year does the Visconti castle house the city’s Archaeological Museum?
A) 1531  B) 1804  C) 1920  D) 1909

30. Stadio del Lido. Until which year was Visconti Castle the seat of the administrative governor and the District Court?
A) 1909  B) 1804  C) 1920  D) 1928
