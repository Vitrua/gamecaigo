The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa di San Siro. The church of S. Siro in Canobbio is first mentioned in which year?
A) 712  B) 863  C) 1335  D) 1472

2. Canobbio Paese. Canobbio is first mentioned in 712 as "Canobli". During the Middle Ages it was a farm of an abbey in which city?
A) Pavia (S. Pietro in Ciel d’Oro)  B) Como (S. Abbondio)  C) Lugano (S. Caterina)  D) Milan (S. Ambrogio)

3. Canobbio Ganna. According to swisstopo’s 2026 boundary data, how large is the municipal area of Canobbio?
A) 54 hectares  B) 128 hectares  C) 310 hectares  D) 760 hectares

4. Mercato Resega. Canobbio is home to a well-known commercial attraction. Which?
A) A casino  B) A ski lift  C) A shopping centre  D) A fishing harbour

5. Davesco Ponte di Valle. In which year was the younger of Canobbio’s two historic mills built (for the De Marchi family)?
A) 1848  B) 1712  C) 1900  D) 1472

6. Canobbio Cioss. Canobbio belongs to which circolo (district subdivision) of the district of Lugano?
A) Vezia  B) Carona  C) Ceresio  D) Agno

7. SUPSI health. Which Russian banker built the Castello di Trevano near Canobbio in 1870 as a "temple to music"?
A) Count Tolstoy  B) Baron Alfred Escher  C) Baron Paul von Dervies  D) Prince Yusupov

8. Canobbio Corba. In 1472 the village church of Canobbio separated from the mother church of which city?
A) Como  B) Lugano  C) Milan  D) Bellinzona

9. Asilo Canobbio. What plant is shown on Canobbio’s coat of arms (white on a red field)?
A) A hemp plant  B) A vine  C) A chestnut tree  D) A rose

10. Belvedere. Which settlement, first mentioned in 1335 with its church of San Silvestro, belongs to Canobbio?
A) Gandria  B) Vezia  C) Conago  D) Davesco

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-B, 4-C, 5-A, 6-A, 7-C, 8-B, 9-A, 10-C
