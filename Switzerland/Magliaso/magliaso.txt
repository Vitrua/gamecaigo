The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa dei Santi Biagio e Macario. In which year was the current parish church of Magliaso built?
A) 1033  B) 1668  C) 1863  D) 1907

2. Castello di San Giorgio. Which schismatic bishop of Como was captured in the castle of San Giorgio, triggering the ten-year war between Como and Milan (1118-1127)?
A) Giovanni Maria Castoreo  B) Karl Konrad von Beroldingen  C) Jost Müller  D) Landolfo da Carcano

3. Giovanni Battista Quadri memorial stele. Magliaso is first mentioned in documents as "de Maliacis". In which year?
A) 854  B) 1033  C) 769  D) 1117

4. Magliaso railway station. Magliaso station is on which railway line?
A) Gotthard railway  B) Lugano-Ponte Tresa railway  C) Lugano-Cadro railway  D) Monte Generoso railway

5. Casa del golf La Magliasina. Which German architect and designer built the former golf clubhouse on Via Vedeggi (before 1929) for the Golf Club Lugano?
A) Fritz August Breuhaus de Groot  B) Mario Botta  C) Dolf Schnebli  D) Orfeo Amadò

6. Cimitero di Magliaso. Magliaso lies on the alluvial plain of which stream?
A) Cassarate  B) Vedeggio  C) Tresa  D) Magliasina

7. Castellaccio. Magliaso consists of the main village and which lake-shore hamlet?
A) Castellaccio  B) Gandria  C) Cantine  D) Brusata

8. Lido di Magliaso. According to swisstopo’s 2026 boundary data, how much of Magliaso’s area is lake surface?
A) 12 ha  B) 176 ha  C) 120 ha  D) 66 ha

9. Lakeside bench on Via Muraglione. Villa La Radura, a modernist villa on Via Muraglione, was completed in which year?
A) 1905  B) 1910  C) 1943  D) 1977

10. Scuola Elementare di Magliaso. Which architect, together with Bernardo Ramelli, designed Magliaso’s kindergarten (1904)?
A) Giuseppe Bordonzotti  B) Mario Botta  C) Dolf Schnebli  D) Orfeo Amadò

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-C, 4-B, 5-A, 6-D, 7-A, 8-D, 9-C, 10-A

Sources and fun facts

1. Chiesa dei Santi Biagio e Macario: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliaso (Chiesa dei Santi Biagio e Macario, eretta nel 1668)
   Fun fact: Karl Konrad von Beroldingen, who helped build the church, also had the castle ruins turned into a sumptuous palace.
2. Castello di San Giorgio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliaso (Storia)
   Fun fact: The castle is documented since 1033; its tower carries a fresco of that era, one of the few examples of Romanesque secular painting in Switzerland.
3. Giovanni Battista Quadri memorial stele: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliaso (Storia)
   Fun fact: In the Lombard era a farm of the monastery of San Pietro in Ciel d’Oro in Pavia stood here.
4. Magliaso railway station: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliaso (Infrastrutture e trasporti: stations Magliaso and Magliaso Paese)
   Fun fact: The same line also serves Agno, Bioggio and Caslano.
5. Casa del golf La Magliasina: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliaso (Monumenti e luoghi d’interesse: Casa del golf La Magliasina, Via Vedeggi 38)
   Fun fact: The building is now closed.
6. Cimitero di Magliaso: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliaso (Geografia fisica)
   Fun fact: The village sits at 287 m above sea level on the lake shore.
7. Castellaccio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliaso (Storia)
   Fun fact: Many mills once stood along the village canal.
8. Lido di Magliaso: swisstopo swissBOUNDARIES3D 2026-01 (BFS 5193): total 176 ha, lake 66 ha
   Fun fact: Magliaso is one of the smallest municipalities of the Malcantone.
9. Lakeside bench on Via Muraglione: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliaso (Villa La Radura (1943), architect Orfeo Amadò)
   Fun fact: Villa Ivy nearby (1910) is in Art Nouveau (Liberty) style.
10. Scuola Elementare di Magliaso: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliaso (Monumenti: Scuola dell’infanzia del 1904)
   Fun fact: Bordonzotti also designed Villa Sussiego (1905-1908) in Magliaso.
