The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Cappella di Cremignone. Cappella di Cremignone: Sorengo consists of the village of Sorengo with the village sections of Cremignone and which other?
A) Pazzallo  B) Cortivallo  C) Barbengo  D) Sassello

A) 1298  B) 1566  C) 1653  D) 1776

3. Cortivallo Paese bus stop. Cortivallo Paese bus stop: the settlement of Cortivallo kept partial independence until which century?
A) The 18th century  B) The 14th century  C) The 16th century  D) The 20th century

A) 1934  B) 1920  C) 1950  D) 1986

7. Franklin University Switzerland. Franklin University Switzerland: since which year has this American private university been in Sorengo?
A) 1920  B) 1934  C) 1965  D) 1986

8. Church of Santa Maria Assunta. Church of Santa Maria Assunta: the church dates back to about the end of which century, but is not mentioned until 1298?
A) The 9th century  B) The 11th century  C) The 13th century  D) The 15th century

A) A Benedictine abbey  B) A hospital  C) A Capuchin monastery  D) A silk mill
