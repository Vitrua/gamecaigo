The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Cappella di Cremignone. The votive chapel has a 19th-century fresco of the Madonna with St Anthony Abbot and which other saint?
A) Saint Rocco B) Saint Dominic of Guzmán C) Saint Francis D) Saint Charles Borromeo

2. Municipio di Sorengo. In June 2025 Sorengo became the first municipality in Ticino to receive which title?
A) Città dell’energia Gold B) Swiss Smart Village C) Cittàverde Svizzera D) Comune solare

3. Area Cisterna. The former recreation centre “Cisterna” is planned to become what?
A) A shopping mall B) A multi-storey car park C) A sports stadium D) A green space open to residents

4. Cappella Clinica Sant’Anna. The clinic’s chapel (1964) has stained glass by which artist?
A) Giovanni Segantini B) Alberto Giacometti C) Emilio Maria Beretta D) Paul Klee

5. Franklin University Switzerland. It is a liberal arts university accredited in...
A) Switzerland and Japan B) Italy only C) Germany and Austria D) Switzerland and the USA

6. Scuola elementare. Sorengo had 591 residents in 1950. About how many in 1990?
A) Around 900 B) Around 1,700 C) Around 3,500 D) Around 6,000

7. Cimitero e cappella. The cemetery chapel keeps a Crucifix fresco from 1843 by which painter?
A) Bernardino Luini B) Pietro Chiesa C) Giovanni Battista Sertorio D) Giovanni Serodine

8. Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta. The parish church is first attested in 1298. When was it built?
A) 15th century B) 18th century C) 20th century D) 11th century

9. Sorengo Laghetto. Sorengo sits on a hill between Lake Lugano and which small lake?
A) Lago di Origlio B) Lago di Muzzano C) Lago di Piano D) Lago di Cadagno

10. Parco giochi Bambini. In which year was Sorengo first mentioned in documents?
A) 1089 B) 1189 C) 1289 D) 1389
