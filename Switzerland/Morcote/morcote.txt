The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Church of Santa Maria del Sasso. In which century was the church of Santa Maria del Sasso first built?
A) 11th century  B) 13th century  C) 15th century  D) 17th century

2. Parco Scherrer. Parco Scherrer was laid out in which year?
A) 1892  B) 1953  C) 1976  D) 1930

3. Church of San Rocco. When was the church of San Rocco built?
A) 1548–1553  B) 1462–1470  C) 1591  D) 1750

4. Castle ruins. Around which year was Morcote’s castle, now in ruins, built?
A) 926  B) 1422  C) 1100  D) 1623

5. Cantine. Besides tourism and crafts, which farming activity has Morcote lived on since the 19th century?
A) Silk farming  B) Dairy farming  C) Viticulture (wine growing)  D) Chestnut milling

6. Casa Anziani. Franchina Rusca founded the Istituto Caccia-Rusca in Morcote. What kind of institution is it?
A) A home for elderly residents  B) A school of drawing  C) A hospital  D) An orphanage

7. Arbostora lakeside park. Casa Chiattone (1953) in the Arbostora area was designed by which architect?
A) Mario Botta  B) Luigi Snozzi  C) Livio Vacchini  D) Mario Chiattone

8. Vedo. Morcote is first documented in which year, in the phrase "habitator in Murcao"?
A) 793  B) 926  C) 1422  D) 1517

9. Burò. How many houses were submerged in the lake by a landslide in 1862?
A) Two  B) Twelve  C) Seven  D) Thirty

10. La Canva. In 2016 Morcote was elected what?
A) Swiss village of the year for gardens  B) The most beautiful village in Switzerland  C) The best-preserved lake port  D) Swiss wine village of the year

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B

Sources and fun facts

1. Church of Santa Maria del Sasso: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Architetture religiose: eretta nel XIII secolo, ricostruita 1462 e 1758)
   Fun fact: It was rebuilt in 1462 and again in 1758.
2. Parco Scherrer: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Parco Scherrer, realizzato nel 1930)
   Fun fact: It was commissioned by Hermann Arthur Scherrer.
3. Church of San Rocco: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Oratorio di San Rocco, 1548-1553)
   Fun fact: The Torre del Capitano nearby is a medieval tower of the 14th century, lowered in the 18th. The Sant’Antonio oratory dates from 1676.
4. Castle ruins: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Ruderi del castello, circa 1100)
   Fun fact: In the early Middle Ages it watched over lake traffic.
5. Cantine: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Storia: turismo, artigianato e viticoltura)
   Fun fact: Until the Melide causeway of 1847, Morcote was the largest port on Lake Lugano.
6. Casa Anziani: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Storia: casa di riposo)
   Fun fact: This question is tied to the stop only by theme; the institute’s exact site was not verified.
7. Arbostora lakeside park: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Casa Chiattone, località Arbostora)
   Fun fact: Another villa in the area, Villa Angela, was built between 1892 and 1914.
8. Vedo: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Storia)
   Fun fact: The name probably comes from Latin "muricatum", meaning walled.
9. Burò: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Storia: nel 1862 sette case finirono sommerse)
   Fun fact: In the plague year 1432, only seven families survived in the village.
10. La Canva: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morcote (Storia: villaggio più bello della Svizzera, 2016)
   Fun fact: In 2023 the UN World Tourism Organization listed it among the "best tourism villages".
