The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Church on Piazza San Cristoforo. Grancia’s parish church (S. Carpoforo, per Wikipedia) probably stands on the foundations of an older church. In which century was it built?
A) 9th century  B) 12th century  C) 16th century  D) 19th century

2. Grancia Paese. Grancia is first mentioned in documents as "in loco Grancie" in which year?
A) 769  B) 1309  C) 1033  D) 1608

3. Molini di Grancia. The grain was ground in mills along which plain’s river?
A) Pian Scairolo  B) Piano di Magadino  C) Pian San Giacomo  D) Val Colla

4. Grancia Centro Commerciale 2. Which kind of shops now surround Grancia, built in the late 20th century?
A) Craft workshops  B) Vineyards  C) Large shopping malls  D) Open-air markets

5. Playground Via Cantonale at Centro Lugano Sud. According to swisstopo’s 2026 boundary data, how large is Grancia?
A) 17 hectares  B) 61 hectares  C) 140 hectares  D) 480 hectares

6. Sasso della Fornace. Which kind of factory opened in Grancia in the 19th century?
A) A brick factory  B) A watch factory  C) A silk mill  D) A paper mill

7. Sasso della Volpe. According to tradition, Grancia had the largest what in the region in the Middle Ages?
A) Cheese dairy  B) Wine cellar  C) Smithy  D) Granary

8. Via Cantonale, southern entrance of Grancia. Until which year was Grancia part of the municipality of Carabbia?
A) 1608  B) 1670  C) 1825  D) 1912

9. Playground Strada Alta. Which monastery’s religious institution had authority over Grancia at the end of the 13th century?
A) San Pietro in Ciel d’Oro (Pavia)  B) S. Abbondio (Como)  C) Disentis  D) S. Maria in Torello (Carona)

10. Premolino. Grancia’s parishes broke away from the parish of S. Pietro in Pambio in which year?
A) 1309  B) 1608  C) 1670  D) 1825

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-C, 2-B, 3-A, 4-C, 5-B, 6-A, 7-D, 8-C, 9-D, 10-B
