The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Church of San Pietro. Church of San Pietro (Maroggia): the parish church of St. Peter is first mentioned in which year?
A) 1644  B) 1579  C) 1731  D) 1906

2. Chapel of Madonna della Cintura. Chapel of Madonna della Cintura: on the foundation of a building from which century was this pilgrimage chapel built in 1731–66?
A) The 11th century  B) The 13th century  C) The 14th century  D) The 16th century

3. Maroggia lakeside park. Maroggia lakeside park: a Roman-era stele was discovered at Maroggia in which year?
A) 1926  B) 1878  C) 1905  D) 1798

4. Church of Santi Vitale e Agata. Church of Santi Vitale e Agata (Rovio): in which year was the parish church first mentioned?
A) 852  B) 1517  C) 1213  D) 1613

5. Oratory of San Vigilio. Oratory of San Vigilio: in which century was this romanesque oratory built?
A) The 9th century  B) The 13th century  C) The 11th century  D) The 15th century

6. Chiesa-oratorio di Santa Maria Assunta. Chiesa-oratorio di Santa Maria Assunta: Rovio is first mentioned in 852 under which name?
A) Rovi  B) Roveo  C) Robio  D) Rovigo

7. Cascata del Botto. Cascata del Botto: how high is the Swiss side of the peak of Monte Generoso, which rises above Rovio?
A) 1,204 m  B) 1,404 m  C) 1,904 m  D) 1,704 m

8. Fountain Piazza G. Motta. Fountain Piazza G. Motta: the modern village of Melano is first mentioned in 799 as what?
A) Melanum  B) Mellani  C) Mellano  D) Melane

9. Church of Sant’Andrea. Church of Sant’Andrea (Melano): in which years was the parish church of St. Andrew totally rebuilt?
A) 1634–1637  B) 1840–1843  C) 1846–1850  D) 1992–1994

10. Sanctuary of the Madonna del Castelletto. Sanctuary of the Madonna del Castelletto: this sanctuary was built in 1634–37 on a previous building from which century?
A) The 12th century  B) The 14th century  C) The 15th century  D) The 16th century

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B
