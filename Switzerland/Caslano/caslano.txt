4. ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: Monte Caslano is today a natural park attached to the mainland by the river’s deposits.
5. ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: It stands midway between the parish church and Santa Maria del Rosario and was restored in 1978.
6. ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: It lies along the cantonal road at the end of the route from the Chiesuola di mezzo.
7. en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caslano (single source)
   Fun fact: In 1604 the Treaty of Varese awarded a disputed area to Caslano, against the Italian municipality of Lavena Ponte Tresa.
8. ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: The locality is first mentioned in 1126, during a clash between the troops of Como and Lodi.
9. en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caslano (single source)
   Fun fact: Caslano borders several Italian municipalities, including Lavena Ponte Tresa and Brusimpiano.
