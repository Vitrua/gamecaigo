The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa di San Cristoforo. In which year was the parish church of San Cristoforo built, on the site of an earlier 14th-century church?
A) 1620  B) 1653  C) 1335  D) 1093

2. Piazza Lago fountain. According to the ISOS inventory, fishing rights on the lake belonged to the episcopal church of which city?
A) Milan  B) Lugano  C) Bergamo  D) Como

3. Museo della Pesca. In which year was the current building of the Museo della pesca inaugurated?
A) 1993  B) 1653  C) 2010  D) 1978

4. Foce della Magliasina. The Magliasina delta filled a narrow strait that once made which hill an island?
A) Monte Caslano  B) Monte Brè  C) Monte San Salvatore  D) Monte Ceneri

5. Cappella di Santa Maria delle Grazie. In which year is the chapel of Santa Maria delle Grazie, called "Chiesuola di mezzo", first cited?
A) 1126  B) 1335  C) 1620  D) 1093

6. Chiesa di Santa Maria del Rosario. In which hamlet of Caslano does the church of Santa Maria del Rosario stand?
A) Torrazza  B) Magliasina  C) Stremadone  D) Carabietta

7. Torrazza park. Torrazza was the site of bitter fighting between the Swiss Confederation and which duchy?
A) Savoy  B) Modena  C) Parma  D) Milan

8. Monte Caslano panoramic viewpoint. In which year did Caslano become a political commune?
A) 1335  B) 1126  C) 1578  D) 1620

9. Biblioteca comunale, Museo della fotografia. In 1604 the Treaty of Varese ruled against which Italian municipality and awarded the disputed area to Caslano?
A) Luino  B) Porto Ceresio  C) Lavena Ponte Tresa  D) Cremenaga

10. Drinking-water fountain, Via Stremadone. According to the ISOS inventory, what is the Stremadone "quartiere" known for?
A) Fishermen’s houses  B) Wine cellars  C) Lime kilns  D) Clay quarries

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-C, 4-A, 5-D, 6-B, 7-D, 8-A, 9-C, 10-B

Sources and fun facts

1. Chiesa di San Cristoforo: ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf; en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caslano
   Fun fact: Remains of the earlier church survive as the sacristy.
2. Piazza Lago fountain: ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: Anyone who wanted to fish had to pay the church a lease.
3. Museo della Pesca: museums.ch https://www.museums.ch/it/visita-al-museo/musei/musei/museo-della-pesca-1966.html (museum opened 1993; current building inaugurated 2010)
   Fun fact: The museum is an external section of the Museo del Malcantone and stands on the lakeshore near the village square.
4. Foce della Magliasina: ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: Monte Caslano is today a natural park attached to the mainland by the river’s deposits.
5. Cappella di Santa Maria delle Grazie: ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: It stands midway between the parish church and Santa Maria del Rosario and was restored in 1978.
6. Chiesa di Santa Maria del Rosario: ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: It lies along the cantonal road at the end of the route from the Chiesuola di mezzo.
7. Torrazza park: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caslano (single source)
   Fun fact: In 1604 the Treaty of Varese awarded a disputed area to Caslano, against the Italian municipality of Lavena Ponte Tresa.
8. Monte Caslano panoramic viewpoint: ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: The locality is first mentioned in 1126, during a clash between the troops of Como and Lodi.
9. Biblioteca comunale, Museo della fotografia: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caslano (single source)
   Fun fact: Caslano borders several Italian municipalities, including Lavena Ponte Tresa and Brusimpiano.
10. Drinking-water fountain, Via Stremadone: ISOS (Federal Inventory of Swiss Heritage Sites) Caslano, https://api.isos.bak.admin.ch/ob/3834/doc/ISOS_3834.pdf
   Fun fact: It is a cluster of cellars in a woodland clearing, reached by a narrow road.
