The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa dei Santi Bernardino e Martino. Which Formula 1 driver is buried in Porza’s cemetery?
A) Jo Siffert  B) Clay Regazzoni  C) Clay Shaw  D) Marc Surer

2. Museo Villa Pia. Casa Soldati in Porza is a residence of the local family of artists and scholars from which century?
A) 14th century  B) 19th century  C) 12th century  D) 17th century

3. Cappella di San Rocco. Porza’s Cappella di San Rocco was built in which period?
A) Late Middle Ages  B) Early Baroque  C) The 19th century  D) The Roman era

4. Viewpoint Via Pian del Bosco. Four Iron Age tombs were found in 1907-1908 during construction of which railway?
A) Gotthard  B) Lugano-Ponte Tresa  C) Lugano-Tesserete  D) Monte Generoso

5. Porza Belvedere. Porza is first mentioned around which years?
A) 1297-99  B) 724  C) 984  D) 1441

6. Porza Tavesio. What is the Ticino-dialect name of Porza?
A) Purza  B) Porscia  C) Puriza  D) Porzò

7. Parco SUPSI. In 1871 a villa was built on the foundations of a medieval castle on the hill north-east of Porza. Which castle?
A) Castello di Montebello  B) Castello di Morcote  C) Castello Visconteo  D) Trevano castle

8. Playground Via alla Monda. For how many years was Pio Regazzoni, father of the F1 driver, mayor of Porza?
A) 12  B) 28  C) 35  D) 8

9. Playground Via Chiosso. A former farm in Porza’s hamlet Cornaredo (Bizzozero) keeps a sundial from which year?
A) 1714  B) 1909  C) 1836  D) 1578

10. Park near the Resega area. Porza’s parish church of Santi Bernardino e Martino was built in which century?
A) 11th century  B) 16th century  C) 18th century  D) 20th century

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-A, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B

Sources and fun facts

   Fun fact: The Soldati family counted artists and scholars among its members.

   Fun fact: The parish church itself dates from the 16th century.

   Fun fact: The Lugano–Tesserete railway was closed in 1967 (it.wikipedia, Tesserete).
5. Porza Belvedere: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porza (History)
   Fun fact: The Benedictine abbey of San Pietro in Ciel d’Oro owned land here in the Middle Ages.
6. Porza Tavesio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porza (header: in dialetto ticinese Purza)
   Fun fact: Porza lies north of Lugano.
7. Parco SUPSI: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porza (History)
   Fun fact: The villa was demolished in 1961 and replaced by a school that is now part of SUPSI, the university of Italian Switzerland.
8. Playground Via alla Monda: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porza (Amministrazione: ventotto anni)
   Fun fact: His son Gianclaudio "Clay" Regazzoni also rests in Porza.
9. Playground Via Chiosso: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porza (Monumenti: meridiana del 1836)
   Fun fact: Sundials are a typical feature of Ticino’s rural buildings.
10. Park near the Resega area: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porza (Monumenti: eretta nel XVI secolo)
   Fun fact: It honours St Bernardino of Siena and St Martin of Tours.
