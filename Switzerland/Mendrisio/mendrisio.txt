The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Church of Santi Cosma e Damiano. A Roman plaque dedicated to which man is built into Mendrisio’s bell tower?
A) Gaius Plinius  B) Publio Valerio Dromone  C) Lucius Verus  D) Marcus Aurelius

2. San Giovanni complex. Santa Maria delle Grazie, in the San Giovanni complex, dates from which year?
A) 1466  B) 1518  C) 1621  D) 1268

3. Casa Croci. In which year was Casa Croci built?
A) 1875  B) 1719  C) 1853  D) 1912

4. Palazzo Turconi. Palazzo Turconi, built 1853–1860 as a hospital, was designed by which architect?
A) Mario Botta  B) Domenico Fontana  C) Luigi Fontana  D) Luigi Snozzi

5. Church of San Sisinio alla Torre. The place name "alla Torre" recalls a medieval castle of which family?
A) The Visconti family  B) The Sanseverino family  C) The Della Torre family  D) The Beroldingen family

6. Mendrisio railway station. Since 2018 Mendrisio has been the end of a rail line to Varese, and from 2019 it reaches which airport?
A) Milan Malpensa  B) Milan Linate  C) Bergamo Orio al Serio  D) Zurich

7. Church of San Martino. In which year is the name Mendrisio first attested, in the form "Mendrici"?
A) 926  B) 1424  C) 1516  D) 793

8. Dated fountain Via alle Cantine. The dated fountain in Via alle Cantine carries the Roman numerals M.DCCC.XLII. Which year is that?
A) 1482  B) 1842  C) 1824  D) 1942

9. Mendrisio Cimitero. The 1516 peace treaty that gave the Mendrisiotto to the Swiss Confederation was signed where?
A) Bern  B) Zurich  C) Fribourg  D) Lugano

10. Drinking fountain Via della Selva. Since 1983 Mendrisio hosts an annual popular event called what?
A) The Carnevale Ambrosiano  B) The Palio di Mendrisio  C) The Festa dell’Uva  D) The Sagra del Borgo

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B

Sources and fun facts

1. Church of Santi Cosma e Damiano: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendrisio (Storia, Età antica). The tower is not identified in the source, so the link to this church is thematic
   Fun fact: It shows that Romanised local elites lived here.
2. San Giovanni complex: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendrisio (Architetture religiose: Santa Maria delle Grazie, 1268)
   Fun fact: Santa Maria in Borgo dates from 1518 and the Capuchin church from 1621.
3. Casa Croci: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendrisio (Architetture civili: Casa Croci, 1875)
   Fun fact: The same architect designed Villa Argentina (1873–1874) in a historic park.
4. Palazzo Turconi: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendrisio (Palazzo Turconi, neoclassico, Luigi Fontana)
   Fun fact: Since 1996 it has housed the Accademia di architettura.
5. Church of San Sisinio alla Torre: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendrisio (Architetture civili; Storia)
   Fun fact: The Beroldingen held the post of Landscriba from 1645 to 1798.
6. Mendrisio railway station: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendrisio (Infrastrutture: Ferrovia Mendrisio-Varese)
   Fun fact: The line connects the Swiss and Lombard rail networks.
7. Church of San Martino: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendrisio (Medioevo)
   Fun fact: The name probably derives from a Lombard personal name.
8. Dated fountain Via alle Cantine: OSM node 4316724136, inscription tag
   Fun fact: Mendrisio’s coat of arms (a silver cross on red) derives from that of Como.
9. Mendrisio Cimitero: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendrisio (Periodo milanese: Pace di Friburgo, 1516)
   Fun fact: The Mendrisiotto was conquered by the Confederates in 1512.
10. Drinking fountain Via della Selva: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendrisio (Eventi)
   Fun fact: The Historic Processions of Holy Thursday and Good Friday are also famous.
