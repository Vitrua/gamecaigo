The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa di San Maurizio. The church of San Maurizio in Bioggio is first mentioned in which year?
A) 1146  B) 1261  C) 1335  D) 1580

2. Palazzo Rusca. Which historic palace is today the town hall (municipio) of Bioggio?
A) Palazzo Rusca  B) Palazzo Riva  C) Casa Staffieri  D) Casa Balestra

3. Chiesa di Sant'Ilario. Restoration of the oratory of Sant’Ilario in 1992 revealed a wooden place of worship from which centuries?
A) 3rd-4th century  B) 7th-8th century  C) 5th-6th century  D) 11th-12th century

4. Chiesa di Sant'Abbondio. Since which year is the parish church of Sant’Abbondio in Bosco Luganese attested?
A) 1261  B) 1146  C) 1835  D) 1580

5. Oratorio San Rocco. Iseo is the highest part of Bioggio. At about how many metres above sea level does it reach?
A) 280 m  B) 314 m  C) 685 m  D) 450 m

6. Oratory at Piazzetta Oratorio. The name of the hamlet Gaggio comes from the Lombard word "gahage". What does it mean?
A) Enclosed and protected land  B) Torrential stream  C) Chestnut grove  D) Water mill

7. Oratorio Madonna delle Grazie. In 1991 a 116 cm trunk fragment found at Mulini di Bioggio suggested what kind of prehistoric settlement?
A) Celtic necropolis  B) Roman rural villa  C) Medieval mill  D) Pile-dwelling settlement

8. Bioggio Molinazzo station. Bioggio Molinazzo is a station on which railway line?
A) Monte Generoso railway  B) Gotthard railway  C) Lugano-Cadenazzo railway  D) Lugano-Ponte Tresa railway

9. Drinking-water fountain, Pianazzo. The former coat of arms of Bosco Luganese showed a tree with how many leaves?
A) Five  B) Seven  C) Nine  D) Twelve

10. Drinking-water fountain, Cimo. Which former municipality’s old coat of arms contributed the vertical sword in Bioggio’s current coat of arms?
A) Iseo  B) Cimo  C) Bosco Luganese  D) Mulini di Bioggio

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-A, 3-B, 4-D, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-D, 9-C, 10-B

Sources and fun facts

1. Chiesa di San Maurizio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioggio (first mentioned 14 September 1261; excavations in 1997-1998 found a church of the 5th-6th century)
   Fun fact: Digs from 1962 also revealed a Roman rural villa of the 3rd century AD, and a path now leads through the archaeological area.
2. Palazzo Rusca: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioggio (Architetture civili)
   Fun fact: Casa Staffieri, a Renaissance house on the cantonal road, was demolished in 1980.
3. Chiesa di Sant'Ilario: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioggio (Età medievale)
   Fun fact: Sant’Ilario stands on a hill overlooking the village; the present church dates to the 16th century.
4. Chiesa di Sant'Abbondio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosco_Luganese (single source)
   Fun fact: Bosco Luganese joined Bioggio in 2004 after a consultative vote on 30 September 2004.
5. Oratorio San Rocco: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioggio (Geografia fisica: 685 m at Iseo, 280 m at Mulini di Bioggio)
   Fun fact: Iseo is the last former municipality to join Bioggio, in April 2008.
6. Oratory at Piazzetta Oratorio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioggio (Origini del nome)
   Fun fact: The nearby torrent Riana, by contrast, takes its name from the Latin rivus + ana.
7. Oratorio Madonna delle Grazie: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioggio (Preistoria)
   Fun fact: It suggests the plain where Lugano airport now stands was once flooded by the lake in Neolithic times.
8. Bioggio Molinazzo station: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioggio (Infrastrutture e trasporti)
   Fun fact: Bioggio is also served by the Lugano Vedeggio station of the Gotthard railway.
9. Drinking-water fountain, Pianazzo: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioggio (Simboli)
   Fun fact: The nine-leaf tree was kept in the new Bioggio coat of arms after the 2004 merger.
10. Drinking-water fountain, Cimo: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioggio (Simboli)
   Fun fact: The same lily appears for both old Bioggio and Cimo.
