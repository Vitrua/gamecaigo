The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Parish church of Santo Stefano. Under which original dedication is the parish church, now called Santo Stefano, attested from 810?
A) San Vitale  B) San Nicolao  C) San Michele  D) San Rocco

2. Oratorio di San Vitale. The oratory of San Vitale is documented between 780 and 810. In which century does that fall?
A) 10th century  B) 12th century  C) 15th century  D) 8th century

3. Oratorio di San Michele. The Romanesque chapel of S. Michele was later rebuilt in which style?
A) Baroque  B) Gothic  C) Neoclassical  D) Art Nouveau

4. Drinking fountain Stráda da Valmára. On which plateau valley does Arogno stand?
A) Val Colla  B) Val Capriasca  C) Val Mara  D) Valle di Muggio

5. Sasso Grosso. In which year did the Manzoni family open the first watch factory in Ticino at Arogno?
A) 1798  B) 1861  C) 1873  D) 1907

6. Cà Nova. The chapel of Cà Nova is dedicated to which subject?
A) The Holy Trinity  B) Saint John Bosco  C) The Annunciation  D) Saint Sebastian

7. Fountain Arogno - Caprino. Repeated territorial disputes between Arogno and the Italian enclave of Campione were settled in which year?
A) 1798  B) 1873  C) 1888  D) 1861

8. Oratorio della Beata Vergine delle Grazie. The oratory of the Beata Vergine delle Grazie stands close to the border crossing to which Italian village?
A) Campione d’Italia  B) Lanzo d’Intelvi  C) Porlezza  D) Cernobbio

9. Playground Area di svago Laura Scotti. On 24 February 1798 Arogno joined which short-lived republic?
A) The Helvetic Republic  B) The Cisalpine Republic  C) The Republic of Riva San Vitale  D) The Republic of Lugano

10. Sighignola viewpoint. Roughly what share of Arogno’s area is forest?
A) About 12%  B) About 85%  C) About 35%  D) About 60%

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B

Sources and fun facts

1. Parish church of Santo Stefano: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (History) and it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno
   Fun fact: In 1581 it broke from the mother church of Riva San Vitale to form its own parish.
2. Oratorio di San Vitale: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (Architetture religiose)
   Fun fact: The village itself is first attested in a document of 1 August 962.
3. Oratorio di San Michele: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (History)
   Fun fact: It lies on the eastern side of the valley, on the road towards Rovio.
4. Drinking fountain Stráda da Valmára: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (Territorio)
   Fun fact: The Val Mara runs along the road to the Italian border near Lanzo d’Intelvi.
5. Sasso Grosso: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (Storia)
   Fun fact: Watchmaking brought many families from French-speaking Switzerland to Arogno.
6. Cà Nova: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (Cappella di Cà Nova, 15th century, reworked in the Baroque period)
   Fun fact: Its frescoes date from the late 15th century.
7. Fountain Arogno - Caprino: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (History)
   Fun fact: Campione d’Italia lies only about 2 km from Arogno.
8. Oratorio della Beata Vergine delle Grazie: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (Architetture religiose)
   Fun fact: It is a Baroque oratory with chamfered corners on its façade.
9. Playground Area di svago Laura Scotti: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (History)
   Fun fact: Ticino became a canton of Switzerland in 1803.
10. Sighignola viewpoint: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arogno (Geography: 84.5% forested, 1997 statistics)
   Fun fact: Only about 12% is used for agriculture.
