The 30 questions, 4 answers each

1. Castelgrande. Since which year have Bellinzona’s three castles been a UNESCO World Heritage Site?
A) 1983  B) 2000  C) 1993  D) 2008

2. Collegiata dei Santi Pietro e Stefano. Bellinzona’s coat of arms shows a silver biscione on red. Which Milanese dynasty is that symbol linked to?
A) The Sforza  B) The Rusca  C) The Hohenstaufen  D) The Visconti

3. Castello di Montebello. Which family built the castle of Montebello around the end of the 13th century?
A) The Rusca family  B) The Visconti family  C) The Sanseverino family  D) The Orelli family

4. Castello di Sasso Corbaro. A tower that became the nucleus of Sasso Corbaro was built after the death of which Visconti duke?
A) Filippo Maria Visconti  B) Francesco Sforza  C) Gian Galeazzo Visconti  D) Ludovico Sforza

5. Bellinzona railway station. The first sections of the Gotthard railway linking Bellinzona to Biasca and Locarno opened in which year?
A) 1847  B) 1882  C) 1874  D) 1907

6. Church of Santi Giovanni Battista ed Evangelista. The church of Santi Giovanni Battista ed Evangelista was built in which year, to a design by Gaetano Matteo Pisoni?
A) 1762  B) 1237  C) 1499  D) 1878

7. Palazzo Franscini. Until which year did Bellinzona, Lugano and Locarno take turns being capital of Ticino?
A) 1803  B) 1814  C) 1907  D) 1878

8. Bagno Pubblico. Bellinzona joined the Swiss Confederation on 14 April 1500 under the joint administration of which three cantons?
A) Zurich, Bern and Lucerne  B) Uri, Schwyz and Nidwalden  C) Uri, Glarus and Zug  D) Schwyz, Obwalden and Basel

9. Church. The Franciscan convent of San Francesco stands next to which church?
A) San Biagio  B) San Sebastiano  C) Santa Maria delle Grazie  D) Sant’Andrea

10. Church of San Biagio. The parish church of San Biagio in Ravecchia is documented from which year?
A) 1002  B) 1237  C) 1173  D) 1500

11. Obelisk. The name Bellinzona is first attested in 590 by which author?
A) Gregory of Tours  B) Pliny the Elder  C) Paulus Diaconus  D) Otto of Freising

12. Teatro sociale. Bellinzona’s Teatro sociale is a rare example in Switzerland of which style?
A) Baroque opera house  B) Art Nouveau  C) Gothic revival  D) Italian neoclassical theatre

13. Church of Santi Quirico e Giulitta. The church of Santi Pietro martire, Quirico e Giulitta is mentioned from which year?
A) 590  B) 1173  C) 1237  D) 1762

14. Church of San Sebastiano. The church of San Sebastiano has origins in which style?
A) Baroque  B) Neoclassical  C) Renaissance  D) Romanesque

15. Church of the Sacro Cuore. Since which year has the conventual church of the Sacro Cuore been a national historic monument?
A) 1986  B) 1874  C) 1907  D) 2000

16. MACT contemporary art museum. In which year did Bellinzona merge with Giubiasco, Sementina, Monte Carasso and other neighbouring municipalities?
A) 2000  B) 2008  C) 2017  D) 2021

17. Parco Greina. After the 2017 merger, the municipal area grew from 19 km² to almost how many km²?
A) 65 km²  B) 95 km²  C) 165 km²  D) 250 km²

18. Church of Sant’Andrea. The church of Sant’Andrea dates from which century?
A) 16th century  B) 10th century  C) 13th century  D) 19th century

19. FFS railway workshop. At the Battle of Arbedo on 30 June 1422, troops of which cantons were defeated by Milan?
A) Zurich and Bern  B) Schwyz and Glarus  C) Lucerne and Zug  D) Uri and Obwalden

20. Church of Santa Maria della Neve. The Murata, a long wall built across the Ticino valley, dates from which century?
A) 9th century  B) 14th century  C) 11th century  D) 17th century

21. Giubiasco fountain. At what altitude is Bellinzona’s town centre?
A) 140 m  B) 440 m  C) 240 m  D) 740 m

22. Oratory of San Girolamo. From 1814 to 1878 the cantonal capital rotated among three towns. How often did it change?
A) Every year  B) Every six years  C) Every three years  D) Every ten years

23. Fortini della Fame. The Fortini della Fame are fortifications from which century?
A) 19th century  B) 11th century  C) 14th century  D) 16th century

24. Church of San Bernardo. Between 1907 and 1972 Bellinzona was linked by rail to which valley?
A) Valle Maggia  B) Val Blenio  C) Valle Verzasca  D) Val Mesolcina

25. Church of the Trinità. Under which Roman emperor was a fort first built in the Bellinzona area, in the late 1st century BC?
A) Nero  B) Augustus  C) Trajan  D) Diocletian

26. Park in Carasso. In 1499 the French troops of which king captured Bellinzona?
A) Francis I  B) Charles VIII  C) Napoleon III  D) Louis XII

27. Viewpoint Carrale Boné. From 568–570 which people used Bellinzona as a permanent garrison?
A) The Longobards  B) The Ostrogoths  C) The Franks  D) The Byzantines

28. Biblioteca comunale. In 1798 Bellinzona became capital of the canton of Bellinzona within which state?
A) The Cisalpine Republic  B) The Duchy of Milan  C) The Helvetic Republic  D) The Swiss Confederation of 1500

29. Monte Carasso park. Bellinzona’s carnival Rabadan has taken place for more than how many years?
A) 50  B) 100  C) 150  D) 300

30. Via Caratti artwork. The Lepontic word "belitio", possible source of the name Bellinzona, means what?
A) Juniper  B) Fortress  C) Ford  D) Marsh
