The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa di Sant’Ambrogio. The church of Sant’Ambrogio in Ponte Capriasca is attested from which year?
A) 1078  B) 1356  C) 1473  D) 1609

2. Ponte Capriasca Paese. The church of Sant’Ambrogio holds a "Last Supper" painted in the style of which master?
A) Caravaggio  B) Giotto  C) Titian  D) Leonardo da Vinci

3. Playground Via Savanone. What is the Ticino-dialect name of Ponte Capriasca?
A) Punt Cavriasca  B) Pont Cavrasca  C) Ponte Cavriaschi  D) Punt Capriasch

4. Ponte Capriasca Nogo Sopra. Rural and bourgeois houses in Ponte Capriasca date from which centuries?
A) 10th-11th centuries  B) 13th-14th centuries  C) 16th-17th centuries  D) 19th-20th centuries

5. Ponte Capriasca Nogo. How far from the village lies the municipality’s distant exclave?
A) About 1 km  B) About 3 km  C) About 10 km  D) About 40 km

6. Parco sorgente ruscello. Which stream’s upper valley forms the exclave of Ponte Capriasca?
A) Serdena  B) Cassarate  C) Vedeggio  D) Maggia

7. Ponte Capriasca Crano. The exclave reaches the border with which Italian municipality?
A) Menaggio  B) Luino  C) Porlezza  D) Cavargna

8. Formigaio. Ponte Capriasca belongs to which district?
A) Locarno  B) Lugano  C) Mendrisio  D) Bellinzona

9. Canavee. Ponte Capriasca’s main church dates from which architectural period?
A) Baroque  B) Renaissance  C) Romanesque  D) Neoclassical

10. Orio. Which saint, patron of Milan, is the main church of Ponte Capriasca dedicated to?
A) Saint Charles Borromeo  B) Saint Ambrose  C) Saint Rocco  D) Saint Martin

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B

Sources and fun facts

1. Chiesa di Sant’Ambrogio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (Monumenti)
   Fun fact: The church dates back to the Romanesque period.
2. Ponte Capriasca Paese: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (Monumenti: Ultima Cena by a "leonardesque" 16th-century painter)
   Fun fact: The painting is known locally as "the cenacle church" (a book is titled "La chiesa del cenacolo").
3. Playground Via Savanone: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (header)
   Fun fact: The municipality belongs to the district of Lugano.
4. Ponte Capriasca Nogo Sopra: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (Monumenti)
   Fun fact: Many remain in the village centre.
5. Ponte Capriasca Nogo: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (Geografia fisica)
   Fun fact: The exclave is a high mountain valley.
6. Parco sorgente ruscello: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (Geografia fisica)
   Fun fact: It runs from Monte Segor to Monte Gazzirola.
7. Ponte Capriasca Crano: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (Geografia fisica)
   Fun fact: Monte Gazzirola sits on that border.
8. Formigaio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (header)
   Fun fact: It lies in the small side valley of the Vedeggio.
9. Canavee: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (Monumenti: risalente al periodo romanico)
   Fun fact: The church was later enriched by a 16th-century Last Supper.
10. Orio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponte_Capriasca (Monumenti: Chiesa di Sant’Ambrogio)
   Fun fact: The nearby church of San Rocco is a separate building.
