The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Collegiata di San Vittore. Collegiata di San Vittore: Balerna is first mentioned in 1115 under which name?
A) Balerno  B) Barerna  C) Valerna  D) Baleria

2. Museo del caffè. Museo del caffè: in which year did the Mendrisio electric tramway, linking Riva San Vitale to Chiasso via Balerna, open?
A) 1874  B) 1798  C) 1950  D) 1910

3. Ruins of Pontegana castle. Ruins of Pontegana castle: in 1416 the Duke of Milan completely separated Balerna from which city?
A) Como  B) Lugano  C) Bergamo  D) Pavia

4. Magazzini Generali. Magazzini Generali: in 1475 the Sforza family separated Balerna administratively from which town?
A) Chiasso  B) Lugano  C) Mendrisio  D) Bellinzona

5. Fondazione Archivio del Moderno. Fondazione Archivio del Moderno: in 1512 Balerna passed to which power?
A) The French crown  B) The Duchy of Savoy  C) The Swiss Confederation  D) The Republic of Venice

6. Chiesa-Oratorio di Sant’Antonio da Padova. Sant’Antonio da Padova: the decree of Aarau (30 May 1798) combined Balerna with Mendrisio in which state?
A) The Helvetic Republic  B) The Cisalpine Republic  C) The Duchy of Milan  D) The Kingdom of Sardinia

7. Scuola Media library. Scuola Media library: in 1798 Balerna refused to become part of which republic?
A) The Helvetic Republic  B) The Venetian Republic  C) The Ligurian Republic  D) The Cisalpine Republic

8. Togna viewpoint. Togna viewpoint: what is the area of Balerna, roughly?
A) 0.6 km²  B) 2.6 km²  C) 6.2 km²  D) 26 km²

9. Playground Via Fontana. Playground Via Fontana: Balerna’s pieve covered the Mendrisio district except Chiasso, which belonged to the pieve of which place?
A) Riva  B) Agno  C) Zezio  D) Locarno

10. Cimitero di Bisio. Cimitero di Bisio: from the 9th century Balerna belonged to the count of which place?
A) Lugano  B) Seprio  C) Locarno  D) Bellinzona

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-A, 7-D, 8-B, 9-C, 10-B
