The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa-oratorio di San Martino. The church-oratory of San Martino was rebuilt in the 18th century on foundations dating from which period?
A) 1st-2nd century  B) 7th-8th century  C) 11th-12th century  D) 15th-16th century

2. Chiesa di Santa Maria Annunziata. The church of Santa Maria Annunziata is attested from which year?
A) 1473  B) 1571  C) 1686  D) 1616

3. Villa Negroni-Morosini. Which institution is housed today in Villa Negroni (formerly Villa Morosini)?
A) Centro di Studi Bancari  B) Museo cantonale  C) Biblioteca cantonale  D) Conservatorio

4. Casa Rossi, Via San Gottardo 18. Casa Rossi (1956), Casa Boillat (1970-72) and Casa Graf were designed by which architect?
A) Mario Botta  B) Peppo Brivio  C) Franco Ponti  D) Livio Vacchini

5. Vezia Villa Recreatio. Vezia is attested under the name "Vescia" in which year?
A) 1297  B) 1616  C) 1473  D) 1653

6. Vezia Marnigo. In which year did Vezia become an autonomous parish?
A) 1473  B) 1571  C) 1686  D) 1653

7. Cadempino Ronchetto. In 1686 Vezia was freed from its duty to help maintain which cathedral?
A) San Lorenzo in Lugano  B) Como cathedral  C) Sant’Ambrogio in Milan  D) San Vittore in Locarno

8. Casa Boillat, Via ai Ronchi 33. Casa Boillat, a wooden house designed in 1970-72, has which central feature?
A) A glass dome  B) A central chimney  C) A flat green roof  D) A rotating façade

9. Vezia Villa Negroni. Which family originally owned the villa now called Villa Negroni, and probably helped Vezia gain privileges in the early 17th century?
A) Morosini  B) Quadri  C) Beroldingen  D) Rusca

10. Office building Via San Gottardo 9. Which architect designed the striped grey-and-black concrete-brick office building at Via San Gottardo 9 (1983-84)?
A) Franco Ponti  B) Rudy Hunziker  C) Dolf Schnebli  D) Aurelio Galfetti

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-C, 6-D, 7-A, 8-B, 9-A, 10-B
