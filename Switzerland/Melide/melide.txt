The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Quirico e Giulitta. Which architect, born in Melide, rebuilt the parish church of Santi Quirico e Giulitta around 1590?
A) Matteo Castelli  B) Domenico Fontana  C) Francesco Borromini  D) Carlo Maderno

2. Parco Tosi. What symbol appears on Melide’s coat of arms?
A) A golden anchor on a blue field  B) A silver fish on red  C) A golden bell on green  D) A white tower on blue

3. Lido di Melide. According to swisstopo’s 2026 boundary data, how much of Melide’s area is lake surface?
A) 164 ha  B) 519 ha  C) 353 ha  D) 105 ha

4. Ponte-diga di Melide. Who built the Melide causeway (ponte-diga) between 1844 and 1847?
A) Domenico Fontana  B) Matteo Castelli  C) Pasquale Lucchini  D) Pierre Vuigner

5. Lungolago Mario Gallino drinking fountain. In which year was the Swissminiatur miniature park founded in Melide?
A) 1959  B) 1847  C) 1968  D) 1909

6. Piazza Moretti. In which year was the old hospice of Santo Spirito, where travellers waited to ferry to Bissone, demolished?
A) 1590  B) 1874  C) 1909  D) 1626

7. Cimitero di Melide. Matteo Castelli (c. 1555-1632), born in Melide, was the architect of the king of which country?
A) Spain  B) Sweden  C) Portugal  D) Poland

8. Cantine di Fondo. What was produced in the old factory at Cantine di Fondo?
A) Soap  B) Silk  C) Cement  D) Matches

9. Sentée du Piancón viewpoint. Melide’s village stretches partly along the slopes of which mountain?
A) Monte Generoso  B) Monte Brè  C) Monte Arbostora  D) Monte San Salvatore

10. Bus stop "Melide, Villa Magnolia". Which municipality borders Melide to the south?
A) Bissone  B) Carona  C) Vico Morcote  D) Paradiso

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-A, 3-C, 4-C, 5-A, 6-C, 7-D, 8-A, 9-C, 10-C

Sources and fun facts

1. Chiesa parrocchiale dei Santi Quirico e Giulitta: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melide (Monumenti e luoghi d’interesse; Storia)
   Fun fact: Fontana (1543-1607) worked at the papal court in Rome and then at the royal court in Naples.
2. Parco Tosi: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melide (Simboli: d’azzurro, all’ancora codata, d’oro)
   Fun fact: Melide’s history of ferrying travellers across the lake fits the anchor symbol (not stated in the source).
3. Lido di Melide: swisstopo swissBOUNDARIES3D 2026-01 (BFS 5198): total 519 ha, lake 353 ha
   Fun fact: Melide’s land area alone is only about 164 hectares, mostly woodland (it.wikipedia).
4. Ponte-diga di Melide: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melide (Monumenti e luoghi d’interesse)
   Fun fact: The village also sits on a moraine point, unique of its kind, jutting halfway across the lake toward Bissone.
5. Lungolago Mario Gallino drinking fountain: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melide (Storia: founded by Pierre Vuigner and Georges Wütrich)
   Fun fact: It shows Switzerland’s main historical and landscape monuments in scale models.
6. Piazza Moretti: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melide (Storia)
   Fun fact: Its building later housed a distillery, before being demolished.
7. Cimitero di Melide: en.wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matteo_Castelli; it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melide
   Fun fact: Castelli is known for his work in Rome and in Poland (early Baroque).
8. Cantine di Fondo: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melide (Storia)
   Fun fact: Cantine di Fondo lies at the southern, lake-side end of the village.
9. Sentée du Piancón viewpoint: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melide (Geografia fisica)
   Fun fact: The municipal territory rises from 273 to 670 m above sea level.
10. Bus stop "Melide, Villa Magnolia": it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melide (Geografia fisica: Lugano N, Carona W, Vico Morcote S, Bissone E)
   Fun fact: Carona is now a quarter of Lugano.
