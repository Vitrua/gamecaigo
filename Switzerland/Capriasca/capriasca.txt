The 10 questions, 4 answers each

1. Chiesa prepositurale di Santo Stefano, Tesserete. Tradition says Countess Grassa gave land to the inhabitants of Tesserete in her will, to atone for the murder of the provost of which church?
A) Sant’Ambrogio  B) Santo Stefano  C) San Martino  D) Sant’Abbondio

2. Casa museo Luigi Rossi. Tesserete is famous throughout Italian-speaking Switzerland for which carnival?
A) Rabadan  B) Carnevale Ambrosiano  C) Fasnacht  D) Or Penagin

3. Scuola Media Tesserete. In which year did Tesserete get its middle school (Scuola media)?
A) 1982  B) 1844  C) 1909  D) 1967

4. Tesserete Al Ponte. The Lugano–Tesserete railway was completed in 1909. In which year was it closed?
A) 1934  B) 1952  C) 1967  D) 1982

5. Oratorio di San Sebastiano. Tesserete’s area was already settled in which prehistoric period, as graves near the parish church show?
A) Stone Age  B) Iron Age  C) Bronze Age  D) Neolithic

6. Chiesa di Sant’Andrea. In 1976 which municipality was merged into Tesserete (with the hamlet of Odogno)?
A) Cagiallo  B) Lopagno  C) Campestro  D) Vaglio

7. Convento di Santa Maria del Bigorio. Capriasca rises northwards to the heights of Monte Bar and which other mountain?
A) Monte Generoso  B) Monte Brè  C) Monte Tamaro  D) Monte Bigorio

8. Pieve church, Vaglio. Capriasca takes its name from the "pieve" of which religious rite?
A) Roman rite  B) Ambrosian rite  C) Orthodox rite  D) Waldensian rite

9. Cagiallo Paese. In which year was the municipality of Capriasca established through a merger?
A) 2001  B) 1976  C) 1982  D) 2008

10. Oratorio di San Carlo Borromeo, Lugaggia. In 2008 Capriasca was enlarged by which three former municipalities?
A) Origlio, Comano and Isone  B) Mezzovico, Vira and Sigirino  C) Bidogno, Corticiasca and Lugaggia  D) Cadro, Sonvico and Dino

Answer key (don’t scroll further until you’ve played): 1-B, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-B, 6-C, 7-D, 8-B, 9-A, 10-C

Sources and fun facts

1. Chiesa prepositurale di Santo Stefano, Tesserete: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesserete (Storia: testamento della contessa Grassa, 1078)
   Fun fact: The countess is buried next to the rectory; the 1078 mention of Tesserete is of doubtful authenticity.
2. Casa museo Luigi Rossi: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capriasca and it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesserete (Cultura)
   Fun fact: It is among the biggest carnivals of the canton.
3. Scuola Media Tesserete: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesserete (Storia)
   Fun fact: A drawing school opened in 1844 and a kindergarten the year after.
4. Tesserete Al Ponte: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesserete (Storia)
   Fun fact: The line brought growth to the settlement.
5. Oratorio di San Sebastiano: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesserete (Storia)
   Fun fact: Steles with inscriptions in a northern Etruscan alphabet were also found.
6. Chiesa di Sant’Andrea: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesserete (Storia)
   Fun fact: Tesserete is the capital of Capriasca.
7. Convento di Santa Maria del Bigorio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capriasca (Geografia fisica)
   Fun fact: Its convent of Santa Maria del Bigorio is a well-known spiritual site (see bibliography).
8. Pieve church, Vaglio: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capriasca (Origini del nome)
   Fun fact: A pieve was the territorial parish of the early Middle Ages.
9. Cagiallo Paese: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capriasca (Storia)
   Fun fact: The seat is in Tesserete.
10. Oratorio di San Carlo Borromeo, Lugaggia: it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capriasca (Storia)
   Fun fact: The first merger in 2001 involved Cagiallo, Lopagno, Roveredo, Sala Capriasca, Tesserete and Vaglio.
