Sources and fun facts

1. HLS "Agno" https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/it/articles/002130/ (documents of 735 and 818); it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agno_(Svizzera)
   Fun fact: Agno was the seat of an ancient pieve that once stretched from Bironico to Marchirolo (pergamena di Sessa, 1352).
2. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agno_(Svizzera) (Oratorio dei Santi Rocco e Carlo)
   Fun fact: From 1626 the oratory also carries the title of San Carlo, hence its double name.
3. it.wikipedia https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agno_(Svizzera) (single source)
   Fun fact: It is preceded by a characteristic 17th-century churchyard (sagrato) and flanked by Palazzo Quadri.
4. swisstopo swissBOUNDARIES3D 2026-01 (BFS 5141): total 303 ha, lake 54 ha; HLS Agno (lake access at the end of an arm of Lake Ceresio)
   Fun fact: Agno lies at the northern end of an arm of Lake Lugano, which the HLS calls Lake Ceresio.
5. Wikipedia "Agno railway station" https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agno_railway_station; it.wikipedia "Stazione di Agno"
   Fun fact: The station is on the metre-gauge Lugano–Ponte Tresa line and is a short walk from the airport.
6. HLS "Agno" https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/it/articles/002130/
   Fun fact: Reclaiming the Piano d’Agno created a large agricultural area from the old river plain.
7. HLS "Agno" https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/it/articles/002130/; Wikipedia "Lugano Airport"
   Fun fact: The airport is shared by the municipalities of Agno, Bioggio and Muzzano.
